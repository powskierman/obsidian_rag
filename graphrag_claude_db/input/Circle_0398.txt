### Notes
- A circle in the xy-plane, centred at P(x0,y0) with radius r is the set of points (x, y) satisfying the equation $$(x−x0)^2+(y−y0)^2 =r^2$$
	- Example:  circle with origin P(2,1) and 3 units
	- $(x-2)^2+(y-1)^2 = 3^2$

- Any  to the circle is perpendicular to the radius joining the centre of the circle to the intersection point.
