### Notes
- calibre, version 7.26.0
WARNING: E-book covers: Amazon has broken display of covers for books sent to the Kindle by USB cable. To workaround it, you have to either keep your Kindle in Airplane mode, or:
Send the books to the Kindle
Disconnect the Kindle and wait for the covers to be deleted by Amazon
Reconnect the Kindle and calibre will restore the covers.
After this the covers for those books should stay put. Click here for details.
