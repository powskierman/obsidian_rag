### Notes
- #### Pairing
	- To pair your White Series 2-1 Switch, put your hub in pairing mode and press the config / favorites button 3x. The LED Bar should start to pulse blue and will turn green if successful.
- #### Factory Resetting

- If the switch is unsuccessful during pairing, you can factory reset it.
- To reset your switch, hold down on the top of the paddle (on) and the config/favorites button simultaneously for 20 seconds until the LED Bar (D) turns red. It will blink red three times to confirm the switch has been reset as shown in the video below.
