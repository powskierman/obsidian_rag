### Notes
-  Quadratic comes from "square"
- A quadratic expression (in x) has the form $ax^2 + bx + c$ where x is a variable and a, b and c are constants such that a is nonzero 
- Most basic quadratic expression: $y=x^2$ where $a=1$ , $b=0$ and $c=0$ 
- Every curve expressed by a quadratic expression is a parabola
- To see where curve is positioned, complete the square
- !
- $x^2+4x+4$ results in a parabola shifted 2 units to the left.  Why?
	- because $x^2+4x+4$ = $(x+2)^2$ therefore $x=-2$
- !
- If $a < 0$, parabola is upside down:
- !
- Parabola crosses x line at the point where factorization = 0
	- $y=-4x^2-8x+12$
	- $0=-4x^2-8x+12$
	- $4(-x+1)(x+3)=0$
	- $x=1, x=-3$
- To shift the curve up, add constant to $f(x)$
	- $y=x^2+4$
- !
- Conversely, to shift down, subtract constant from expression
- To shift right, complete the square to factor the expression and subtract constant from $x$
- !
