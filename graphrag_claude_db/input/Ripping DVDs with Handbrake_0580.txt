# Ripping DVDs with Handbrake
Date: 2025-02-12

### Notes
-  To rip your Great Courses videos using HandBrake, follow these detailed step-by-step instructions with optimal settings:
**Preparation**
	1.	Install HandBrake on your computer if you haven’t already.
	2.	Insert the Great Courses DVD into your computer’s DVD drive.
**Step-by-Step Instructions**
Step 1: Open HandBrake and Select Source
	1.	Launch HandBrake.
	2.	Click “Open Source” in the top-left corner.
	3.	Select your DVD drive from the list of available sources.
	4.	HandBrake will scan the DVD. This may take a few minutes.
Step 2: Choose Title and Chapters
	1.	In the “Title” dropdown, select the main feature. Usually, this is the longest title.
	2.	If you want to rip specific lectures, you can select individual chapters.
Step 3: Select Output Settings
	1.	In the “Presets” sidebar, choose “Fast 480p30” for standard definition DVDs.
	2.	Set the output format to MP4 in the “Format” dropdown menu.
Step 4: Adjust Video Settings
	1.	In the “Video” tab:
	•	Encoder: H.264 (x264)
	•	Framerate: Same as source
	•	Constant Quality: RF 20 (adjust between 18-22 for SD content)
	•	Encoder Preset: Slow (for better quality)
Step 5: Adjust Audio Settings
	3.	In the “Audio” tab:
	•	Codec: AAC (avcodec)
	•	Bitrate: 160
	•	Mixdown: Stereo
Step 6: Configure Subtitles (if needed)
	5.	In the “Subtitles” tab, add any subtitle tracks you want to include.
Step 7: Set Destination
	1.	Click “Browse” next to “Save As” to choose where to save your ripped video.
	2.	Name your file appropriately (e.g., “GreatCourses_Lecture1”).
Step 8: Start Encoding
	1.	Click the “Start Encode” button at the top of the window.
	2.	HandBrake will begin ripping and encoding your video. This process may take some time depending on your computer’s speed and the length of the video.
Additional Tips
	•	For batch processing multiple lectures, use the “Add to Queue” button instead of “Start Encode” for each title, then start the queue when you’ve added all desired lectures.
	•	If you encounter any issues with copy-protected DVDs, you may need to install libdvdcss.
	•	Always ensure you have the right to make personal copies of your DVDs before ripping them.
