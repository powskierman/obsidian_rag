### Notes
- Average rate of change of a function
	- If a function $y = f(x)$ is defined for $x$ such that $a ≤ x ≤ b$ then the quotient $f{(b)−f(a)}\over{b−a}$represents the average rate of change of $f(x)$ as $x$ moves from $a$ to $b$.
