- #### New Connections: 
	- Look at this week's reading and ask "What does this remind me of?"
	- Strengthen Existing: Add new insights to existing concept notes
	- Challenge Assumptions: Find books that contradict your current thinking
- #### Monthly Deep Dive:
	- Pick one major concept from your reading
	- Find all books that touch on it
	- Create a synthesis note
	- Look for gaps in your understanding
- #### Quarterly Themes:
	- Review your reading patterns
	- Identify emerging themes
	- Create higher-level connection maps
	- Plan future reading to fill gaps

- #### Practical Techniques
	- Use Obsidian Features:
		- Tags: #psychology #leadership #decision-making
		- MOCs (Maps of Content): Create index pages for major topics
		- Dataview queries: Automatically list related books
		- Graph view: Visualize connections
		- Random note: Discover forgotten connections
	- Connection Prompts:
		"This reminds me of..."
		"This contradicts what I learned in..."
		"This author would agree/disagree with..."
		"I can apply this to my work in..."
		"This explains why..."
	- ##### Example Connection Workflow
		- ###### While Reading:
		When you encounter an interesting idea, ask: "Where have I seen this before?"
		Add a quick link: "This concept of psychological safety also appears in [[Team of Teams]]"
		Make a note to develop this connection later
				- After Finishing a Book:
					- Review your highlights
					- Identify 3-5 key concepts
					- Check if you have concept notes for these ideas
					- Either add to existing concept notes or create new ones
					- Look for books that support or challenge these ideas

		- ###### During Reviews:
			- Look at your graph view for isolated notes
			- Find ways to connect orphaned ideas
			- Strengthen weak connections with more context
			- Prune connections that don't add value
