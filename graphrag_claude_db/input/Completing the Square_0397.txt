### Notes
- To solve $ax2+bx+c=0$ by completing the square:
	- Example: $$x^2−6x-3=0$$
- Transform the equation so that the constant term $c$ is alone on the right side.
	- $$x^2-6x=3$$
- If $a$, the leading coefficient (the coefficient of the $x^2$ term), is not equal to 1, divide both sides by $a$
- Add the square of half the coefficient of the x-term, $\large({b \over 2a})^2$ to both sides of the equation.
	- $$x^2-6x+(-3)^2=3+9$$
- Factor the left side as the square of a binomial.
	- $$(x-3)^2=12$$
- Take the square root of both sides.  (Remember: $(x+q)^2=r$ is equivalent to $x+q=±\sqrt r$)
	- $x-3=\pm\sqrt12$
- Solve for $x$
	- $x=3\pm2\sqrt3$
