Partial. This is a faux spot mode, using roughly 9.4 percent of the image area to calculate exposure, which, as you can see in Figure 4.8, is a rather large spot, represented by a yellow disk. The status LCD icon is shown in the upper-left corner. Use this mode if the background is much brighter or darker than the subject. Spot. This mode confines the reading to a limited area in the center of the viewfinder, as shown in Figure 4.10, making up only 2.3 percent of the image. This mode is useful when you want to base exposure on a small area in the frame, such as a spotlight performer on stage, surrounded by a black background.
