- Germinal centers (GCs) are specialized microstructures located within the B-cell zones (follicles) of secondary lymphoid organs, such as:
	•	Lymph nodes
	•	Spleen
	•	Ileal Peyer’s patches (in the small intestine)
	•	Tonsils and other mucosal-associated lymphoid tissues
