[[Algebra MOC]]

- [Purple Math, Factoring](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/factquad.htm)
- 
- Factoring consists of writing a number or another mathematical object as a product of several factors 
	- 3 × 5 is a factorization of the [integer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer "Integer") 15
-  When factorization = 0, one of the factors = 0
	-  If a and b are real [[Numbers]] then the product ab is zero if and only if a = 0 or b = 0 (including the possibility that a = b = 0).
- When factoring quadratic polynomials, we will need to find two numbers that will multiply to be equal the constant term c, and will also add up to equal b
	- $ax^2+bx+c$
-  "Roots" (or "radicals") are the "opposite" operation of applying exponents
