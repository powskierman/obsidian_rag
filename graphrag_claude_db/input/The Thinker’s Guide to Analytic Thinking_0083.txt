Essential Idea:Reasoning occurs when we draw conclusions based on reasons. We can upgrade the quality of our reasoning when we understand the intellectual processes that underlie reasoning. ([96](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=96)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466051

Your purpose is your goal, your objective, what you are trying to accomplish. We also use the term to include functions, motives, and intentions. ([180](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=180)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466052

Be aware: All of what we do is guided by our purposes or goals. We are aware of only some of our goals. When our goals reflect our greed or possessiveness, or such, we deny them as goals. We then describe our actions in such a way as to hide purposes to which we cannot admit. ([188](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=188)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466053

The question lays out the problem or issue and guides our thinking. When the question is vague, our thinking will lack clarity and distinctness. ([191](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=191)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466054

Often the real question or problem is hidden or obscure. People resist admitting problems that cast them in a negative light. We need intellectual courage to bring the real problems and issues to the surface . ([199](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=199)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466055

Information includes the facts, data, evidence, or experiences we use to figure things out. It does not necessarily imply accuracy or correctness. ([202](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=202)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466056

Be aware: of missing information, especially information that reveals contradictions, hypocrisy, and self-deception on our part. Most people seek only information that supports what they already believe. ([210](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=210)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466057

Inferences are interpretations or conclusions you come to. Inferring is what the mind does in figuring something out. ([213](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=213)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466058

---

Our conclusions are often distorted by our self-serving interests, which disengage our sense of justice. Make sure that your conclusions are based on all the relevant information and that you haven’t excluded information that does not support your preconceptions. ([221](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=221)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466059

Assumptions are beliefs you take for granted. They usually operate at the subconscious or unconscious level of thought. ([224](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=224)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466060

---

The root of problems in thinking often lies with false assumptions. Because assumptions are usually unconscious, they often embody prejudices, biases, stereotypes, and one-sided or false beliefs. Practice explicitly identifying assumptions and checking them for justifiability. ([232](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=232)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466061

---

Concepts are ideas, theories, laws, principles, or hypotheses we use in thinking to make sense of things. ([235](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=235)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466062

---

Be aware: The ways in which we think about the world are determined by our ideas or concepts. Yet these concepts are often twisted in self-serving ways by the mind. We often use concepts to manipulate people or to pursue vested interests. Use language with care, precision, and fairness. ([243](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=243)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466063

Point of view is literally “the place” from which you view something. It includes what you are looking at and the way you are seeing it. ([247](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=247)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466064

---

Be aware: All of reasoning is couched within a point of view. We often fail to consider viewpoints with which we disagree. Why? Because to consider those viewpoints might require us to change our own viewpoint, to give up some beliefs or goals we want to maintain. Realize that one of the hallmarks of the critical thinker is a willingness to enter sympathetically into any and every viewpoint, and then to change one’s views when the evidence warrants a change. ([257](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=257)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466065

Implications are claims or truths that logically follow from other claims or truths. ([261](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=261)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466066

---

Implications follow from thoughts. Consequences follow from actions. ([262](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=262)) %% Color: blue %% ^334466067

---

Be aware: Thinking through the implications of one’s thought prior to acting requires discipline and the ability to think at multiple levels. Every action we take has implications. What is more, we should be aware that once we identify important implications of an act, we should also identify important implications of those implications. Implications are like the concentric circles that radiate outward when a stone is dropped in a pond. ([269](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005XCRHYC&location=269)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466068
