# Apple Watch Ad Hoc Distribution
Date: 2024-04-06

#### Comprehensive guide on how to install a standalone watchOS app using ad hoc distribution.

##### Prerequisites

- Apple Developer Account: You must have an active paid Apple Developer account (Individual or Organization).
    
- Development and Distribution Certificates: Create valid development and distribution certificates within your Apple Developer account.
    
- App ID: A registered App ID specifically for your standalone watchOS app.
    
- Devices: The UDIDs (Unique Device Identifiers) of the Apple Watches you wish to install the app on.
    
- Xcode: Xcode installed on your Mac development machine.
    

##### Steps

1. Create an Ad Hoc Provisioning Profile
    

- Navigate to "Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles" on the Apple Developer website ([https://developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/)).
    
- Click "Profiles" in the sidebar, then click the "+" button to create a profile.
    
- Under "Distribution," pick "Ad Hoc."
    
- Select the App ID associated with your standalone watchOS app.
    
- Choose the distribution certificate you generated earlier.
    
- Add Apple Watches by their UDIDs to the list of devices.
    
- Give your profile a descriptive name (e.g., "MyWatchApp Ad Hoc") and generate it.
    
- Download the generated provisioning profile.
    

2. Prepare Your App for Archiving
    

- In your Xcode project, open the project settings for both the watchOS app target and the WatchKit Extension target.
    
- Under "Signing & Capabilities," ensure the following:
    

- "Team" is set to your developer team.
    
- "Automatically manage signing" is disabled.
    
- "Signing Certificate" for both "Debug" and "Release" is your distribution certificate.
    
- "Provisioning Profile" for both "Debug" and "Release" is set to the newly created Ad Hoc profile.
    

3. Archive and Export
    

- Thoroughly clean your project (Product -> Clean Build Folder).
    
- Create an archive by going to Product -> Archive
    
- In the Xcode Organizer window, select your archive and click "Distribute App."
    
- Choose "Ad Hoc" as the distribution method.
    
- Pick the correct code signing options and your Ad Hoc provisioning profile.
    
- Export your app as an IPA file to a chosen location.
    

4. Distribution  
    There are a couple of ways to distribute your IPA:
    

- Over-the-Air (OTA) Distribution:
    

- Upload the IPA file and a simple HTML manifest file to a web server. (You can find examples of a manifest file online)
    
- Provide users with a link to the HTML file. They can open the link on their iPhone (paired with the Apple Watch) and tap "Install."
    

- Direct Installation with Tools:
    

- Use Xcode's Devices and Simulators window to directly connect your Apple Watch and install the app.
    
- Utilize tools like Apple Configurator 2 for managing installations on multiple devices.
    

Important Notes

- Ad hoc distribution allows installation on a limited number of registered Apple Watches.
    
- Ad hoc provisioning profiles have an expiration date (usually one year).
    
- If you're building an independent watchOS app (not requiring a companion iOS app), ensure it's configured correctly in your Xcode project for standalone functionality.
