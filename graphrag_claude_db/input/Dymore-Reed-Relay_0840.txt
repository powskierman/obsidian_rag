### Notes
- ![Module SU-5AIRIP-N ](Dymore-Reed-Relay-image1.jpg)
-   **Product Introduction:**
-   1.Adopt field-effect tube
-   2.Input port signal compatible with NPN and PNP,PLC devices
-   3.Input signal voltage 3.3V
-   4.Input port adopts photoelectric isolation to ensure the safety of control port;with operating state indicator lamp
-   5.Connection:VCC and GND are power supply ports,please connect with them according to introduction;1+,1- connect with control signal;controlled appliance output port is open-drain output,label as 01
-   6.Module Specification:72*19*18mm
-   
-   Pin Definition:
-   1.Signal Input:1+ is for signal input positive;signal input negative
-   2.Power Supply Input:VCC is for DC power positive;GND is for DC power negative
-   3.Signal Output:01 is for signal output (drain output)
-   
-   Drain Output Picture:
-   When there is control signal,output port 01 and module DC power supply negative are connected to form return circuit
-   
-   Module External Connection Picture:(can control under 30V,5A household appliances)
