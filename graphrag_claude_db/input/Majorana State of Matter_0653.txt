### Notes
- Key Features of the Majorana State in Quantum Computing
1. Topological Superconductivity
	•	Achieved by cooling a hybrid material (indium arsenide and aluminum) to near absolute zero (-400°F) and applying magnetic fields.
	•	Forms nanowires with MZMs (Majorana Zero Modes) at their ends, which store quantum information in a topologically protected state.
2. Stability Advantages
	•	Unlike traditional qubits, MZMs are less prone to decoherence (loss of quantum information when measured).
	•	Information is encoded in the spatial arrangement of particles, making it resilient to local disturbances.
3. Scalability
	•	Microsoft's [Majorana 1](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/blog/quantum/2025/02/19/microsoft-unveils-majorana-1-the-worlds-first-quantum-processor-powered-by-topological-qubits/) currently uses 8 qubits but is designed to scale to 1 million qubits, a threshold expected to unlock revolutionary computational power for AI, energy research, and medicine.
