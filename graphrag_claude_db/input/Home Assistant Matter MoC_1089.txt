- Setting up a new device in Home Assistant
	- 3 controller manufacturers are acceptable.
		- Apple
			- Requires an AppleTV 4K
		- Google
		- Alexa
			- The easiest to use
				- Go to devices
				- Press + to add new device
				- Scroll to the bottom, choose other
				- In other, choose Matter
				- Scan the QR Code.  This will commission the device.
				- Once the device is setup, click on it and test it.
				- Click the gear for settings
				- Click other Assistants and Apps.  A code will be provided.
		- Home Assistant 
			- Go to Home Assistant
					- Settings
					- Integrations
					- Add Integration
					- Add Matter device
					- Choose it's already in use.
					- Choose other controllers
					- Enter the setup code provided by Alexa.
					- If the device is not added:
						- Go to Add-Ons/Matter Server/webUI
						- Check for broken nodes
						- Commission device from here
