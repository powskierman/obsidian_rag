### Notes
[Purple Math, Synthetic Division](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/synthdiv.htm)

- Synthetic division is used for finding zeroes (or roots) of polynomials. 
-  if $x – n$ is a factor, then $x = n$ is a zero, and if $x = n$ is a zero, then $x – n$ is a factor.
-  Example: $y = x^2+ 5x + 6$
	- You can factor the polynomial as $y = (x + 3)(x + 2)$. 
	- Then find the zeroes of _y_ by setting each factor equal to zero and solving. 
	- $x = –2$ and $x = –3$ are the two zeroes of $y$.
- You can also work backwards from the zeroes to find the originating polynomial.
	- Given that _x_ = –2 and _x_ = –3 are the zeroes of a quadratic, 
		- then _x_ + 2 = 0, so _x_ + 2 is a factor, and _x_ + 3 = 0, so _x_ + 3 is a factor. 
		- Therefore, you know that the quadratic must be of the form $y = a(x + 3)(x + 2)$.
