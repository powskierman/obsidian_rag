### Notes
- The UroLift System is a minimally invasive treatment for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), a condition in which the prostate enlarges and obstructs the flow of urine. It is designed to provide symptom relief without cutting, heating, or removing prostate tissue.
##### How UroLift Works
	•	The procedure involves the placement of small implants that lift and hold the enlarged prostate tissue away from the urethra, thereby widening the passage and improving urinary flow.
	•	It is typically performed as an outpatient procedure under local or light sedation and takes about 15–30 minutes.
	•	Patients can usually return home the same day without requiring a catheter, and recovery is quick, with most individuals resuming normal activities within a few days.
##### Benefits
	•	Preservation of Sexual Function: Unlike many other treatments for BPH, UroLift does not negatively impact erectile or ejaculatory function.
	•	Rapid Symptom Relief: Many patients notice improvement in urinary symptoms within two weeks.
	•	Minimally Invasive: The procedure avoids incisions or tissue removal, reducing risks associated with more invasive surgeries like transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP).
	•	Drug-Free Option: UroLift offers a permanent solution without the need for ongoing medication.
##### Limitations
	•	Prostate Size: UroLift is generally recommended for patients with small to moderately sized prostates (up to 80–100 mL).
	•	Complex Anatomy: It may not be suitable for patients with substantial intravesical lobes or certain anatomical variations.
	•	Temporary Side Effects: Some patients experience mild side effects such as blood in the urine, pelvic pain, or urinary urgency, which typically resolve within 2–4 weeks.
Who is a Candidate?
##### UroLift is ideal for men who:
	•	Have moderate BPH symptoms and wish to avoid daily medications.
	•	Are seeking a minimally invasive option with minimal downtime.
	•	Want to preserve sexual function.
While UroLift provides effective relief for many patients, it is not suitable for everyone. Consultation with a urologist is essential to determine whether this treatment is appropriate based on individual prostate size, anatomy, and overall health.
