### Notes
- A polynomial function (or simply polynomial) has the form $p(x) = a_0 + a_1x + a_2x^2 + . . . + a_nx^n$ where $n$ is a nonnegative integer and $a_0, . . . a_n$ are constants, with $a_n \ne 0$. 
- We call $n$ the degree of the polynomial or polynomial function. 
- When $n$ = 0, we get a constant function $p(x) = a_0$.
- When $n$ = 1, we get a  $p(x) = a_0 + a_1x$.
- When $n$ = 2, we get a quadratic function $p(x) = a_0 + a_1x + a_2x^2$.
- When $n$ = 3, we get a cubic function $p(x) = a_0 + a_1x + a_2x^2 + a_3x^3$.
