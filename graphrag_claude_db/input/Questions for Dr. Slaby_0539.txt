#### Bispecific Antibodies Questions:
1. “If bispecific antibody - which one and for how long?”
	1. [[media/Bispecific Antibodies Success Rate#^5f6df0|Glofitamab Efficacy]]
	2. The STARGLO phase III trial demonstrated a clear benefit for [[Glofitamab + GemOx]] in transplant-ineligible patients with relapsed/refractory DLBCL and HGBCL, with a complete response rate of 58.5% versus 25.3% for R-GemOx, and a 41% reduction in risk of death (hazard ratio 0.59, p=0.011). Median overall survival was extended to 25.5 months compared to 12.9 months with standard therapy, and progression-free survival was significantly longer.  
	3. Glofitamab in combination with GemOx is available in Alberta for eligible adult patients with relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) who are not candidates for autologous stem cell transplant. Health Canada granted market authorization for this regimen in August 2025
