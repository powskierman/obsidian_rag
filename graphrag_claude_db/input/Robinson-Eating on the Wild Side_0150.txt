There is a surprisingly simple way to preserve the phytonutrients in lettuce and other greens while they are being stored in your refrigerator. Put the greens in a resealable plastic bag, squeeze out as much air as possible without crushing the leaves, seal the bag, and then use a needle or a pin to prick it with between ten and twenty evenly spaced holes. (Make ten pinpricks in quart-size bags and twenty in larger bags.) Put the bag in the crisper drawer of your refrigerator, which is the coolest, most humid location. — location: [384](kindle://book?action=open&asin=B00A2DVYSM&location=384) ^ref-50921
