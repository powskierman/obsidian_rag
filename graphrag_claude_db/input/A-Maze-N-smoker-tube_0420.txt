# A-Maze-N smoker tube



The A-Maze-N pellet smoker you point to is very good, but I prefer its younger brother, the A-Maze-N Tube pellet smoker (I have both the 6″ and 12″ tubes). I use BBQr's Delight 100% flavorwood pellets (the ones that come in the 1 pound bags). I get 2-3 hours of smoke from the 6″ tube and 5-6 hours with the 12″ tube
