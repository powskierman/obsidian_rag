### Notes
-  Differentials: $dy \over dx$
-  $f'(x) =$$\lim\limits_{h \to 0}$$f(x+h)-f(x) \over h$ <=>$\lim\limits_{h \to 0}$$\frac{\Delta y} {\Delta x}$ <=>$dy \over dx$
- If $y=k$ ->${dy \over dx}=0$
- if $f(x)=mx+b$->${dy \over dx}=m$
- The derivative is [[additive]]
	- ex: $ax^2+bx+c$
		-  $dy \over dx$= ${d \over dx}(ax^2)+{d \over dx}(bx)+{d \over dx}(c)$
