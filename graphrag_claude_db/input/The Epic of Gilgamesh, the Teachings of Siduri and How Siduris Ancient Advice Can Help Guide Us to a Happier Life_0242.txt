the three core concepts of Siduri's philosophy are: Understanding, Joy and Family. ([213](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00B5KFX06&location=213)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465942

---

Siduri teaches us first to try to understand others in our lives, what their motivations are and can we see things from their perspective. If we wish to follow Siduri’s ancient teachings, we should make every effort to live with understanding in our lives, and make sure that our colleagues, friends and family feel that we appreciate their point of view. ([215](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00B5KFX06&location=215)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465943

---

Siduri and Carnegie teach that we should make every effort to find common ground that will genuinely interest us and let us understand the world through the eyes of our friends, colleagues and family. ([222](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00B5KFX06&location=222)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465944
