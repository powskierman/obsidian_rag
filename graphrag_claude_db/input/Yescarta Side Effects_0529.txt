### Notes
- Here's a quantified breakdown of the key risk factors with Yescarta (axicabtagene ciloleucel) therapy for double-hit DLBCL:

### Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS)
- Incidence: 90-93% of patients experience some degree of CRS
- Severe CRS (Grade 3 or higher): 9-13% of patients
- Timing: Typically begins within 1-5 days after infusion
- Duration: Usually resolves within 1-2 weeks
- Mortality risk: <1% directly attributable to CRS

### Neurologic Toxicities (ICANS)
- Incidence: 60-65% of patients experience some neurologic symptoms
- Severe neurotoxicity (Grade 3 or higher): 28-31% of patients
- Timing: Usually begins between days 5-14 after infusion
- Duration: Typically resolves within 2-3 weeks
- Risk of permanent neurologic damage: Very rare (<1%)

### Cytopenias (Low Blood Counts)
- Neutropenia (Grade 3-4): 80-85% of patients
- Thrombocytopenia (Grade 3-4): 40-45% of patients
- Anemia (Grade 3-4): 45-50% of patients
- Prolonged cytopenias (>90 days): 17-24% of patients

### Infections
- ==Overall infection risk: 40-50% of patients within first 3 months==
- ==Serious infections: 20-25% of patients==
- Fatal infections: Approximately 3% of patients

### [[B-cell Aplasia]] & Hypogammaglobulinemia
- Incidence: Nearly 100% of responders experience B-cell depletion
- Duration: Can persist for months to years
- Patients requiring immunoglobulin replacement: 15-25%

### Overall Response Rates for Double-Hit DLBCL
- Complete response: 50-60% of patients
- Partial response: 10-15% of patients
- Duration of response at 12 months: 65-70% of complete responders remain in remission
- Long-term remission (>2 years): Approximately 40-45% of patients

### Treatment-Related Mortality
- Overall risk: 3-5% of patients
- Primary causes: Infections, neurologic complications, and CRS

These numbers represent general statistics based on clinical trials and may vary based on your specific clinical situation, age, disease burden, and comorbidities. Your treatment team will monitor you closely for these complications and intervene promptly if any develop.
