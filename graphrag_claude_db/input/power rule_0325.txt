### Notes
-  Allows us to [[differenciation|differentiate]] expressions of the form $f(x^n)$
- Take the power and multiply it by the [[coefficient]], then you reduce the power by 1.
	- The power goes to the front and is multiplied by the [[coefficient]] then reduced by one
	- $f'(x^a)$ = $d \over dx$`[`$x^a$`]` = $ax ^{a-1}$
		- if $f(x) = x^4$, then $f^′(x) = 4x^3$.
		- If $f(x) = x^{−3}$ then $f^′(x) = −3x^{−4}$. 
		- If $f(x) = x^{1/3}$ then $f^′(x)$ = ${1 \over3}x^{−2/3}$.
		- If $f(x) = x^{2/3}$ then $f^′(x)$ = $2{3 \over \sqrt[3]x}$.
