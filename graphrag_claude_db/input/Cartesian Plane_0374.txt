- Cartesian plane: The Cartesian plane or xy-plane consists of all ordered pairs (x, y) as x and y range over all reals numbers, denoted by:$$\mathbb{R}^{2}=\{(x, y) \mid x, y \in \mathbb{R}\}$$
- View coordinates in space:  Add a 3rd axis Z, which we think of as the new ‘vertical’, which is perpendicular to the xy-plane.$$
\mathbb{R}^{3}=\{(a, b, c) \mid a, b, c \in \mathbb{R}\}
$$

- Line in the plane
	- A line is defined by 2 points
	- $ax+by=c$
		- $x$ and $y$ are variables that define the horizontal and vertical coordinates respectively
		- $a$, $b$ and $c$ are constants
