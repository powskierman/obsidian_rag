### Notes
-  EPIC Crispy Skin Smoked Spatchcock Chicken Recipe
- Smoked spatchcock chicken on the pellet grill makes it tender and juicy with crispy skin. 
- Prep Time
	- 15 minutes mins
- Cook Time
	- 1 hour
- Rest Time
	- 10 minutes

- Ingredients
	- 1 whole chicken
	- 2 tbsp olive oil
	- 3 tbsp BBQ rub or any chicken / poultry dry rub seasoning you’d like
	- 2 tbsp butter salted, melted (optional, for crisping skin toward the end of smoking it)
	- 1 tbsp parsley or fresh herbs of your choice, optional for serving
- Instructions
	- Preheat Traeger Grill to 225 degrees F.
	- Spatchcock whole chicken by flipping it, so the backbone side is up (breast side down) on a large cutting surface. Cut along each side of the backbone, remove and discard it.
	- Flip spatchcocked chicken upside down (breast facing up) and push on the breast, to flatten it out and help it smoke evenly. This should break the breastbone so it lays flat.
	- Season spatchcocked chicken by first patting it dry with paper towel. Then drizzle on melted butter. Lastly, sprinkle seasonings all over the chicken, and rub in the seasoning.
	- Smoke chicken for 30 minutes at 225 degrees F. until internal temperature is 120 degrees F.
	- Place the spatchcocked chicken in the smoker, directly on the grill grates, with breast side up. 
	- Crank the smoker up to 375 degrees F, while leaving the partially smoked spatchcock chicken in there until internal temperature is 150 degrees F.
	- Brush on melted butter (optional step for extra crispy skin) when spatchcock chicken smokes to about 15 degrees LESS than your desired done temp, it’s time to brush on melted butter.
	- For this recipe done temperature is 165 degrees F.
	- Crank the smoker up to 450 degrees F to finish smoking with crispy skin.
	- Remove Traeger spatchcock smoked chicken when the internal temperature reaches 165 degrees F as measured with an internal read thermometer in the thickest part of the chicken thigh. 
	- Finish smoker spatchcocked chicken with a sprinkle of fresh herbs, if desired. Rest for 10 minutes, slice / carve, and enjoy.
