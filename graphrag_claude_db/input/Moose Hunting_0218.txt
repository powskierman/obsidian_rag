# Highlights

A weather forecast that calls for dark skies with a steady or off-and-on drizzle is going to mean hunting all day. Moose move in this type of weather. ([1896](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00KV1MJ7A&location=1896)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465935

---

Rain and drizzle are among the best weather conditions for hunting because the temperatures are normally on the cooler side. Able to move around comfortably, the moose take full advantage of these conditions. ([1898](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00KV1MJ7A&location=1898)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465936

---

Moose are like deer in that when the wind blows hard, they tend to lay low and not move around much. ([1902](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00KV1MJ7A&location=1902)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465937
