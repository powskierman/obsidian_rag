### Notes
-  Welcome to the Cardio-Oncology clinic. It was lovely chatting with you this morning. The Cardio-Oncology clinic provides a surveillance program for patients undergoing potentially cardio-toxic chemotherapy. We monitor your heart function, which is a measurement of the percentage of blood leaving the heart each time it squeezes. Certain medications that you will be receiving for your cancer treatment, can affect your heart function and potentially reduce it. 

To summarize what we discussed on the phone today: 

1. Echocardiograms (ultrasounds of your heart) and lab work will be ordered before chemotherapy, then repeated again at 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, then annually at 2 years, and 3 years. 
2. The clinic will contact you if any imaging and labs are abnormal. If imaging and labs are normal, a letter will be sent to you. 
3. Please monitor yourself for cardiac symptoms, specifically shortness of breath and leg swelling, over the next three years and to contact the clinic if these symptoms appear

If you have any questions or any concerns, please do not hesitate to call the clinic at 403-956-2623. Lama, the clinic booking clerk will be in contact with you shortly with your echocardiogram appointment.
