##### Quantification of Long-Term Effects After High-Dose Chemotherapy and #ASCT
1.	**Infertility**
	•	Prevalence: High-dose chemotherapy causes permanent infertility in most patients. Nearly all men and women treated with alkylating agents or total body irradiation experience gonadal failure, leading to infertility.
2.	**Cataracts**
	•	Prevalence: Total body irradiation increases the risk significantly, with studies reporting cataract development in 20-50% of patients post-#ASCT.
3.	**Cardiovascular Complications**
	•	Congestive Heart Failure (CHF): The 10-year cumulative incidence is 8% in #ASCT recipients compared to 2% in matched populations, with a hazard ratio (HR) of 5.5.
	- Other Conditions: Arrhythmias, pericarditis, and myocardial infarction occur in 2-10% of patients.
4.	**Pulmonary Dysfunction**
	•	Prevalence: Chronic pulmonary complications, such as restrictive lung disease, affect up to 20-30% of survivors due to chemotherapy or radiation exposure.
5.	**Endocrine Disorders**
	•	Thyroid Dysfunction: Hypothyroidism occurs in up to 15-20% of patients post-#ASCT, especially with radiation exposure.
	•	Diabetes: Increased incidence linked to metabolic changes post-treatment.
6.	**Neurological Complications**
	•	Prevalence: Long-term neurotoxicity affects 11-59% of patients, including ==peripheral neuropathy and cognitive impairments==. Severe neurological complications are less common but have been reported in 2.8% of cases.
7.	Bone Disorders (Osteoporosis)
	•	Prevalence: ==Osteoporosis or fractures occur in approximately 20-30% of survivors due to corticosteroid use and hormonal deficiencies post-treatment.==
