### Notes
-  Here’s how to reveal your Battery Cycle count if you use an iPhone <15 series:

- Install the shortcut — **_PowerUtil._**
- Go to _Settings ⚙️ < Privacy & Security ✋ < Analytics & Improvements_.
- Click ‘_Analytics Data’_ below ‘_Share iPhone Analytics’_,
- Find and tap on the (most recent) file whose name starts with _Analytics-20XX_.
- Share it to PowerUtil (by clicking Share icon at the top-right corner)
