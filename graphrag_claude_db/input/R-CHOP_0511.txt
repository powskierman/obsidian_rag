# R-CHOP
Date: 2024-12-10

### Notes
- R-CHOP is the standard initial (front-line) treatment for most patients newly diagnosed with DLBCL. It’s usually given in cycles (every 21 days) for about 6 cycles. This regimen has a proven track record of achieving remission and, in many cases, cure.
- **Side Effects & Considerations:**
- Common side effects include lowered blood cell counts, increased infection risk, fatigue, hair loss, neuropathy (especially from vincristine), and gastrointestinal issues. Most are manageable with supportive care, and long-term outcomes can be very good, especially for patients who achieve complete remission. 
- R-CHOP stands for a combination chemotherapy regimen plus a monoclonal antibody:
	•	Rituximab: A monoclonal antibody against CD20, a protein found on most B-cells, which helps the immune system target lymphoma cells.
	•	Cyclophosphamide: A chemotherapy agent that interferes with DNA replication in rapidly dividing cells.
	•	Hydroxydaunorubicin (Doxorubicin): An anthracycline chemotherapy drug that damages DNA and disrupts cancer cell division.
	•	Oncovin (Vincristine): A plant alkaloid that prevents cancer cells from completing cell division.
	•	Prednisone: A corticosteroid that helps reduce inflammation and can induce lymphoma cell death.

How It Works:
These drugs work synergistically to kill rapidly dividing lymphoma cells. Rituximab, in particular, revolutionized DLBCL treatment by helping the immune system recognize and destroy malignant B-cells more effectively.
