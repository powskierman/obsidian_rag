# lenalidomide
Date: 2024-12-17

### Notes
-  ==Lenalidomide is an oral immunomodulatory drug that has shown promise in treating high-grade B-cell lymphomas (HGBL), particularly those with [[MYC]] rearrangements==, due to its ability to modulate the tumor microenvironment and directly downregulate [[MYC]] and its target genes. It is often studied in combination with standard chemotherapy regimens, such as [[R-CHOP]]
- Clinical Outcomes:
	•	In [[MYC]]-rearranged large B-cell lymphoma (MYC+ LBCL), ==lenalidomide combined with [[R-CHOP]] (R2CHOP) has demonstrated improved outcomes compared to [[R-CHOP]] alone.==
	•	A phase II trial reported a complete metabolic remission (CMR) rate of 67% at the end of treatment, with 2-year overall survival (OS) and event-free survival (EFS) rates of 73% and 63%, respectively.
	•	Meta-analysis data show that adding lenalidomide improves progression-free survival (PFS) significantly (hazard ratio HR = 0.77), though it does not consistently improve overall survival (OS).
	•	==Lenalidomide may also reduce the risk of central nervous system (CNS) relapse due to its ability to penetrate the CNS.==
	3.	Comparison to Intensive Regimens:
	•	==Outcomes with R2CHOP are comparable to those seen with more intensive regimens like DA-EPOCH-R in [[MYC]]-rearranged lymphomas, but R2CHOP may be better tolerated in some patients.==
