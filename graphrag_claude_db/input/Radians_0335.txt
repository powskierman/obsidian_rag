[[Unit Circle]]
[[Trigonometry MOC]]


- A radian is defined as an angle subtended by an arc of the same length as the radius of the circle
- ![[Radian.png|200]]
- Diameter of circle = $2\pi r$
	- 360◦ corresponds to 2π radians, the perimeter of the [[unit circle]].
	- 1 radian ${360 \over 2\pi}={180 \over \pi} {\approx 57.3^\circ}$. 
	- $1^\circ$ corresponds to $2\pi \over 360$=$\pi \over 180$ radians
- The radian measure of an angle θ is the length of an arc on a [[unit circle]] subtended by θ.
