### Notes
- ### Growth and Decay
-  General form of an exponential function is: $y = f(x) = Ae^{(kx)}$
	- A and k are nonzero constants. 
		- A is typically positive and represents initial size or population
		- k represents the growth rate if positive and decay rate if negative
		- x represents time
		- $f(x)$ models:
			- exponential growth when k > 0, 
			- and exponential decay when k < 0.
 
 ### Example
- Find the decay rate of Radon
	- Exponential decay formula
		- $f(x) = Ae^{(-kx)}$
	-  Replace $f(x) with 0.5a and t with 3.8.
		- $0.5a=Ae^{-(k)(3.8)}$
	-  Divide each side by a.
		- $0.5 = e^{-k(3.8))}$
	- Apply logarithm powers property
		- $ln(0.5)=(3.8)k$
	-  Divide each side by –3.8.
		- $-{ln 0.5 \over 3.8} = k$
		- $k = -{ln 0.5 \over 3.8}$
		- $k = 0.1824$
