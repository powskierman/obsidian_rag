# Highlights

If you have a Canon compact DSLR, such as a T2, T3, T4, 7D, Rebel, Kiss, or anything with more than two numbers in the name (like 60D or 600D), you should choose Canon EF-S lenses whenever possible. Canon EF-S lenses are optimized for the compact sensor. ([2254](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2254)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465403

---

Prime lenses are also the best choice for low-light and action photos, such as concerts, wildlife, and some sports, because the prime lenses tend to let in far more light. ([2463](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2463)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465404

---

Lenses with small maximum f/stops, such as f/2.8, allow more light to reach the camera’s autofocus sensors. Therefore, an f/2.8 lens will focus much faster than an f/5.6 lens, especially in low-light. ([2477](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2477)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465405

---

I can’t recommend image stabilization enough. If your budget allows for it, it’s the single most important feature on any lens. Image stabilization will save you countless blurry shots, allow you to use lower ISO settings (and thus reduce the noise in your pictures), and allow you to focus more on composition and lighting than camera settings. ([2503](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2503)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465406

---

Image stabilization still helps, but if you plan to shoot flying birds, a 400mm or 500mm lens without image stabilization can still get the job done. ([2507](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2507)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465407

---

If you need image stabilization that’s not recorded by the on-camera mic, consider the Canon’s STM series of lenses. ([2561](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2561)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465408

---

The Canon STM lenses are designed to be used with video, but currently your options are limited to the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM, the EF-S 18-135mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM (both for compact DSLRs with the smaller APS-C sensor), the EF-S 55-250 f/4-5.6 IS STM, and the EF 40mm f/2.8 STM (for all Canon DSLRs). ([2575](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B00FH827ME&location=2575)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465409
