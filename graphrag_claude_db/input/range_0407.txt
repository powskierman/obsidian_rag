### Notes
- the range is what you end up with.
- the range is all the possible $y$ values.  
- To find the range:
	-  Write down y=f(x) and then solve the equation for x, giving something of the form x=g(y). 
	- Find the  of g(y), and this will be the range of f(x). 
	- If you can't seem to solve for x, then try [graphing the function to find the range.](https://www.expii.com/t/finding-the-domain-and-range-of-a-function-graphically-9974)
	- The range of a  function of the form: $c\sqrt{ax+b}+k$ is $f(x) \ge k$
	- The function range is the combined domain of the inverse functions
	- Find the  of: $2+{1 \over{1-x}}$
		- Inverse of $2+{1 \over{1-x}}$ = ${-x+3}\over {-x+2}$
		- Find the domain of each :
			- Domain of ${-x+3}\over {-x+2}$ = $x<2$ or $x>2$
