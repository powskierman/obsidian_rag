### Notes
- Thermaspec Transurethral Microwave Thermotherapy (TUMT) is a minimally invasive treatment for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) and prostatitis. It uses microwave energy to heat and destroy excess prostate tissue, relieving urinary symptoms caused by an enlarged prostate.
##### How Thermaspec TUMT Works
	•	A specialized catheter with a microwave antenna is inserted through the urethra into the prostate.
	•	Microwaves are emitted to heat the targeted prostate tissue to therapeutic levels, causing cell death and tissue shrinkage.
	•	Cooling fluid circulates around the catheter to protect the urethra from heat damage, and a rectal temperature sensor ensures safety during the procedure.
##### Procedure Details
	•	Outpatient Setting: TUMT is typically performed in a clinic without general anesthesia, using local anesthetics or mild sedation.
	•	Duration: The procedure takes about 30 minutes to 1 hour.
	•	Post-Treatment: Patients may require a urinary catheter for a few days due to temporary swelling of the prostate.
##### Benefits
	1.	Minimally Invasive: No surgery or incisions are required.
	2.	No Hospital Stay: Patients can go home the same day.
	3.	Safe for High-Risk Patients: Suitable for individuals who cannot undergo more invasive procedures due to age or comorbidities.
	4.	Symptom Relief: Over 50% of patients report significant improvement in urinary symptoms, including reduced frequency, urgency, and difficulty urinating.
	5.	Preserves Sexual Function: TUMT has minimal impact on erectile function compared to surgical options like TURP (transurethral resection of the prostate).
##### Limitations
	•	Efficacy Compared to TURP: While TUMT improves symptoms, it is generally less effective than TURP in terms of long-term symptom relief and urinary flow improvement.
	•	Need for Retreatment: Symptom improvements may diminish over time, with retreatment rates ranging from 20–84% within five years.
	•	Temporary Side Effects: These include blood in urine, urinary urgency, or difficulty urinating, which typically resolve within a few weeks.
Ideal Candidates
##### TUMT is recommended for:
	•	Men with moderate BPH symptoms who want a non-surgical alternative.
	•	Patients at high surgical risk due to health conditions or those seeking to avoid anesthesia.
	•	Individuals concerned about preserving sexual function.
##### Risks and Considerations
	•	Possible complications include urinary tract infections, temporary inflammation of the prostate, or difficulty urinating post-procedure.
	•	TUMT may not be suitable for patients with certain conditions, such as urethral strictures, pacemakers, or metal implants in the pelvic area.
Thermaspec TUMT offers an effective and safe option for managing BPH symptoms, particularly for patients seeking a minimally invasive alternative. However, consultation with a urologist is essential to assess individual suitability and discuss potential outcomes.
