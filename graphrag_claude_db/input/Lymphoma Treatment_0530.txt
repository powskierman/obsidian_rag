### Notes
- Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma (DLBCL) is an aggressive type of non-Hodgkin lymphoma originating from B lymphocytes. 
- ==My lab report indicates the detection of a [[MYC]] rearrangement in 51% of the nuclei analyzed==, suggesting clonal evolution. This is a significant finding as ==[[MYC]] rearrangements are associated with aggressive forms of B-cell lymphoma, particularly high-grade B-cell lymphomas (HGBCL)==.
- Furthermore, the FISH report indicates that I have:
	-  ==High-grade B-cell lymphoma with [[MYC]] and [[BCL2]] rearrangements or "double hit lymphoma"== most likely [[Lymphoma/GCB DLBCL]]
- Treatment Options:
	•	==Standard first-line therapy such as [[R-CHOP]]== (rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine, prednisone) ==is often inadequate for [[MYC] rearranged lymphomas due to their aggressive nature.==
	•	Because of my age and heart condition, Dr. [[Lymphoma/Dr. Jiri Slaby|Slaby]]has elected to start me off with a 7 day pretreatment of prednisone, followed by standard [[R-CHOP]].  It's a safer, less intrusive approach that I agree with.
	•	Emerging therapies include ==targeted treatments such as [[lenalidomide]], which modulates [[MYC]] expression and may be used in combination with chemotherapy.==
