# Breyanzi
Date: 2025-01-15

### Backlink
[[Diagnosis and Options]]

### Reference

### Notes
-  Breyanzi (lisocabtagene maraleucel) is a CAR T-cell therapy used to treat certain types of relapsed or refractory non-Hodgkin lymphoma. It is a personalized treatment that uses the patient’s own immune cells, which are genetically modified to target and destroy cancer cells.
#### Indications
Breyanzi is approved for adults with the following conditions:
	•	Large B-cell lymphoma (LBCL), including diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), high-grade B-cell lymphoma, primary mediastinal large B-cell lymphoma (PMBCL), and follicular lymphoma grade 3B (FL3B).
	•	Chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL)/small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL) after at least two prior lines of therapy.
	•	Mantle cell lymphoma (MCL) and follicular lymphoma (FL) after two or more prior systemic treatments.
It is typically used when prior treatments, such as chemotherapy or stem cell transplants, have failed or the cancer has relapsed.
#### How It Works
Breyanzi is a genetically modified autologous T-cell immunotherapy. The treatment process involves:
	1.	Collecting the patient’s T cells through a process called leukapheresis.
	2.	Genetically engineering these T cells in a lab to express a chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) that targets the CD19 protein found on cancerous B cells.
	3.	Reinfusing the modified T cells into the patient to attack and kill cancer cells.
Before infusion, patients undergo preparatory chemotherapy to clear existing white blood cells and create space for the CAR T cells.
#### Efficacy
Clinical trials have shown promising results:
	•	In studies with DLBCL patients who had failed previous treatments, 53% achieved complete remission, and 73% showed at least partial response.
	•	Patients treated with Breyanzi lived longer without disease progression compared to those receiving standard treatments (average of 10.1 months vs. 2.3 months).
#### Administration
Breyanzi is administered as a single intravenous infusion in certified treatment centers. Patients must remain close to the treatment center for at least four weeks post-infusion for monitoring due to potential side effects.
Side Effects
Breyanzi can cause serious, potentially life-threatening side effects, including:
	•	Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS): Symptoms include fever, chills, fast heart rate, low blood pressure, and difficulty breathing.
	•	Neurological Toxicity: Symptoms may include confusion, seizures, or difficulty speaking. Other common side effects include fatigue, nausea, diarrhea, and infections.
To manage risks like CRS, medications such as tocilizumab must be available during treatment.
#### Warnings and Precautions
	•	Breyanzi is not recommended for patients with primary central nervous system lymphoma.
	•	It should only be administered in specialized healthcare facilities equipped to handle severe side effects.
	•	Patients are advised to stay near their treatment center for close monitoring after infusion.
Breyanzi represents a significant advancement in personalized cancer care by leveraging genetic engineering to harness the body’s immune system against aggressive cancers.
