When we divide an hour into 60 minutes, or a minute into 60 seconds, or a full circle into 360 degrees, we’re channeling the sages of ancient Babylon. ([430](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B006R8PL7G&location=430)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334461414

complex numbers. Here “complex” doesn’t mean “complicated”; it means that two types of numbers, real and imaginary, have bonded together to form a complex, a hybrid number like 2 + 3i. ([574](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B006R8PL7G&location=574)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334461415

multiplying by i produces a rotation counterclockwise by a quarter turn. ([583](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B006R8PL7G&location=583)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334461417

---

If we multiply a positive number by i², the corresponding arrow rotates 180 degrees, flipping from east to west, because the two 90-degree rotations (one for each factor of i) combine to make a 180-degree rotation.     But multiplying by –1 produces the very same 180-degree flip. That’s the sense in which i² = –1. ([590](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B006R8PL7G&location=590)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334461418
