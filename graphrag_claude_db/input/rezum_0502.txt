### Notes
- Rezum is a minimally invasive treatment for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), a condition where the prostate enlarges and obstructs urinary flow. It uses water vapor therapy to reduce prostate tissue and alleviate symptoms.
##### How Rezum Works
	•	Rezum delivers steam (water vapor) into the prostate tissue using a small needle inserted through the urethra. The thermal energy from the steam causes controlled cell damage, shrinking the enlarged tissue over time.
	•	The body naturally absorbs and removes the treated tissue through its healing process, typically over about three months.
##### Procedure Details
	•	The procedure is performed in an outpatient setting under local anesthesia or light sedation.
	•	Each steam injection lasts about 10 seconds, and the entire procedure takes approximately 30 minutes.
	•	A urinary catheter is usually required for 3–5 days afterward to manage post-treatment swelling.
##### Benefits
	1.	Minimally Invasive: No incisions or permanent implants are involved.
	2.	Preserves Sexual Function: Unlike some surgical procedures, Rezum has minimal risk of erectile dysfunction or retrograde ejaculation.
	3.	Quick Recovery: Patients can resume normal activities within a few days, with symptom relief often noticeable within two weeks and full effects by three months.
	4.	Long-Term Efficacy: Studies show sustained symptom relief for up to four years, with improvements in urinary flow and reduced urgency and frequency.
##### Limitations
	•	Prostate Size: Suitable for prostates between 30 cm³ and 80 cm³; not recommended for very large prostates (>120 cm³).
	•	Temporary Side Effects: These may include blood in urine or semen, painful urination, and urinary urgency during recovery.
	•	Catheter Use: Most patients require short-term catheterization after the procedure due to swelling.
	•	Not Suitable for All Patients: Exclusions include those with penile prostheses, urinary implants, or recurrent urinary tract infections.
##### Who is a Candidate?
Rezum is ideal for men over 50 with moderate BPH symptoms who want a non-surgical alternative to medications or invasive procedures like TURP (transurethral resection of the prostate). It is particularly appealing to those seeking to preserve sexual function while achieving long-term symptom relief.
Consultation with a urologist is essential to determine if Rezum is appropriate based on individual anatomy and health conditions.
