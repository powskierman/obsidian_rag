### Notes
- PiPiano is an implementation of xpmidi on a Raspberry Pi accessed through remote desktop.  It is used to play midi files on Yamaha Disklavier.
- It is accessed using VNC Viewer at 192.168.0.128:5901
- Double click on PianoPi icon on desktop or in the command bar.  Open the Music directory or load a playlist.
- Instructions are in smb://Jumbo/Applications/RaspberryPi/PiPiano/xpmidi-5.1/xpmidi-5.1/README
