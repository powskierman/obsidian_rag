### Notes
-  Expresses the [derivative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derivative "Derivative") of the [inverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverse_function "Inverse function") of a [bijective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bijective "Bijective") and [differentiable function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differentiable_function "Differentiable function") $f$ in terms of $f'$.
	- The inverse of $f(a)$ is $[f^{-1}]'(a)$ = $1 \over {f'(f^{-1}(a))}$
- The derivative of $f^{−1}$ is the reciprocal of the derivative of $f$, with argument and value reversed.
- To find the inverse function, swap $x$ and $y$, and solve the resulting equation for $x$.
	- If the initial function is not one-to-one, then there will be more than one inverse.
	- wap the variables: 𝑦=𝑒𝑥y=ex becomes 𝑥=𝑒𝑦x=ey.

Now, solve the equation 𝑥=𝑒𝑦x=ey for 𝑦y.

𝑦=ln(𝑥)y=ln⁡(x)
