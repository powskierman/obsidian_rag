Once the add-on is installed, users can select multiple tabs, right-click to access the \"Copy to Clipboard\" menu, and then choose the desired output format. The extension supports custom formats and can be configured via its settings. It is open source and aimed at improving productivity for users who want to save, share, or backup tab information efficiently[5][4]."
