### Notes
-  Independent Watch App Setup: 
	- Double-check that your app is correctly set up as an independent Watch app in Xcode. The app should not be mistakenly configured as a companion app to an iOS application.
	- Bundle Identifier: Ensure that the bundle identifier is unique and correctly configured.
	- Provisioning Profile: Verify that the correct provisioning profile is being used for the app.
-   App disappears from watch
	- In Target/General/Deployment info:
		- Make sure that "supports running without IOS app installation" is checked
		- In App Icons:  make sure that "include all app icon sets" is checked
