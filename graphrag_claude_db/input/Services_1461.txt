1.  [AWS SNS](https://console.aws.amazon.com/sns/v3/home?region=us-east-1#/dashboard)

    a.  a managed messaging service that lets you decouple publishers from subscribers.

    b.  Topics

        i.  a logical access point that acts as a*communication channel*.

        ii. lets you group multiple*endpoints*(such as AWS Lambda, Amazon SQS, HTTP/S, or an email address).

    ```{=html}
    <!-- -->
    ```
    a.  Subscriptions

        i.  To receive messages published to[a topic](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sns/latest/dg/sns-create-topic.html), you must*subscribe*an[endpoint](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sns/latest/dg/sns-create-subscribe-endpoint-to-topic.html#sns-endpoints)to the topic.

        ii. When you subscribe an endpoint to a topic, the endpoint begins to receive messages published to the associated topic.
