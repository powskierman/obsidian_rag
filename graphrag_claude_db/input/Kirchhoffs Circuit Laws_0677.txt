### Notes
The first of of Kirchhoffs laws deals with the current flowing around a closed circuit, **Kirchhoffs Current Law, (KCL)** while the other law deals with the voltage sources present in a closed circuit, **Kirchhoffs Voltage Law, (KVL)**.
