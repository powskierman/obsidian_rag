# BBQ Baby Back Ribs

Remove silver skin from back of ribs;

Add dry rub;

Phase l: Smoke: Cook 2-3 hours at 250 F.

Phase Il: Packetize: in foil 30-60 minutes with spoonful of liquid: Beer, Apple juice White wine

Phase Ill: Sauce: Lather on BBQ sauce cook 20 minutes

Packetize phase is optional; it is used to

create the "fall off the bone" tenderness



Big Green Egg with Plate Setter covered in foil.

Pie tin full of water
