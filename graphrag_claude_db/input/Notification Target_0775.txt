### Notes
-  To set up your iPhone as a notification target in Home Assistant, you need to use the Home Assistant Companion app and configure it to receive notifications. Here are the steps to do this:

#### Step 1: Install the Home Assistant Companion App

1. **Download the Home Assistant Companion app** from the App Store on your iPhone.

2. **Open the app** and log in to your Home Assistant instance using your Home Assistant URL and credentials.

#### Step 2: Configure Notifications in Home Assistant

1. **Open Home Assistant** in a web browser and go to your **Home Assistant dashboard**.

2. **Go to Configuration**:
   - Click on your profile icon or your initials at the bottom of the sidebar.
   - Scroll down to the "Companion App" section.
   - Ensure the "Push ID" is populated. This indicates that the app is registered to receive notifications.

### Step 3: Create a Notification Automation

1. **Create a new automation**:
   - Go to **Settings**.
   - Click on **Automations & Scenes**.
   - Click on the **+ (plus)** icon to create a new automation.

2. **Set up the trigger**:
   - Choose the trigger type that you want to use (e.g., state, time, etc.).

3. **Set up the action**:
   - Choose **Call Service** as the action type.
   - For the service, select `notify.mobile_app_your_iphone_name` (replace `your_iphone_name` with the name of your iPhone as it appears in Home Assistant).
   - In the service data, define your notification message. For example:
     ```yaml
     message: "This is a test notification"
     title: "Notification Title"
     ```

4. **Save the automation**.

### Step 4: Test the Notification

1. **Manually trigger the automation** to test if the notification is sent to your iPhone.

2. **Check your iPhone** to ensure the notification is received.

### Example YAML Configuration

Here is an example of an automation in YAML that sends a notification to an iPhone:

```yaml
alias: Notify iPhone
description: Sends a test notification to iPhone
trigger:
  - platform: state
    entity_id: binary_sensor.door_window_sensor
    to: 'on'
action:
  - service: notify.mobile_app_your_iphone_name
    data:
      message: "The door sensor is triggered!"
      title: "Home Assistant Alert"
mode: single
```

Replace `binary_sensor.door_window_sensor` with your specific sensor entity ID and `your_iphone_name` with your iPhone's name in Home Assistant.

By following these steps, you should be able to set up your iPhone as a notification target in Home Assistant.
