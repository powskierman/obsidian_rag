# Baguettes Recipe

### Notes
-  To make baguettes at home using a poolish, follow this detailed step-by-step procedure. This process involves preparing a poolish the night before, mixing the final dough, shaping the baguettes, and baking them to perfection.

## Ingredients

### Poolish
- **Flour:** 160 grams (about 1.25 cups)
- **Water:** 160 grams (about ½ cup + 3 tablespoons)
- **Instant yeast:** A small pinch (about 1/32 teaspoon)

### Final Dough
- **All of the poolish**
- **Flour:** 320 grams (about 2.5 cups)
- **Malt extract:**  1.25 grams (0.5 teaspoon)
- **Gluten:** 5 grams 
- **Water:** 160 grams (about ½ cup + 3 tablespoons)
- **Salt:** 9 grams (about 1.25 teaspoons)
- **Instant yeast:** 1 to 2 grams (about ½ + 1/8 teaspoon)

## Procedure

### Day 1: Prepare the Poolish
1. **Mix Ingredients:** In a bowl, dissolve the yeast in water, then add the flour. Mix until combined.
2. **Fermentation:** Cover the bowl and let it sit at room temperature for 12-16 hours until bubbles form on top and it begins to wrinkle[1][5].

### Day 2: Make the Dough
1. **Combine Ingredients:** Add water to the poolish to loosen it, then mix with flour, salt, and yeast in a large bowl until all ingredients are combined into a shaggy dough.
2. **Kneading:** Knead the dough until it passes the windowpane test, indicating proper gluten development[1][5].
3. **First Fermentation:** Cover the dough and let it ferment for two hours, performing a stretch-and-fold at the one-hour mark[1].

### Shaping
1. **Divide and Pre-shape:** Divide the dough into three equal pieces and shape them into rounds. Let them rest for about 20 minutes[1].
2. **Shape Baguettes:** Roll each piece into a baguette shape approximately 12 inches long. Place them on a floured couche or tea towel[2][6].

### Proofing
1. **Final Proofing:** Cover the shaped baguettes and let them proof for about 60 to 90 minutes until they have visibly expanded[2][5].

### Baking
1. **Preheat Oven:** Preheat your oven to 460°F (240°C) with a baking stone inside if available.
2. **Score Baguettes:** Just before baking, score each baguette with a sharp blade or lame.
3. **Bake with Steam:** Place baguettes in the oven and add steam by pouring water into a preheated tray at the bottom of the oven. Bake for about 25 minutes until golden brown[1][6].

4. **Cooling:** Remove from oven and allow to cool on a wire rack before slicing.

This method will yield delicious homemade baguettes with a crispy crust and chewy interior, thanks to the poolish preferment technique that enhances flavor and texture[5][6].
