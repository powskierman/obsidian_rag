# Algebraic Identities
[[Algebra MOC]]

- ---
created: 2022-01-20T07:54:53 (UTC -10:00)
tags: []
source: https://brilliant.org/wiki/algebraic-identities/


 ## Excerpt
An algebraic identity is an equality that holds for any values of its variables. For example, the identity $a^2+b^2 = a^2+2ab+b^2$.  

Because of this identity, we can replace any instance of $a^2+b^2$ with $a^2+2ab+b^2$ and vice versa.

### Binomial Theorem Identities
- The following identities are product formulas that are examples of the [[binomial theorem]]
$$
\begin{aligned}
&(x+y)^{2}=x^{2}+2 x y+y^{2} \\
&(x-y)^{2}=x^{2}-2 x y+y^{2} \\
&(x+y)^{3}=x^{3}+3 x^{2} y+3 x y^{2}+y^{3} \\
&(x-y)^{3}=x^{3}-3 x^{2} y+3 x y^{2}-y^{3} \\
&(x+y)^{4}=x^{4}+4 x^{3} y+6 x^{2} y^{2}+4 x y^{3}+y^{4} \\
&(x-y)^{4}=x^{4}-4 x^{3} y+6 x^{2} y^{2}-4 x y^{3}+y^{4} .
\end{aligned}
$$


### [[Factoring]] Identities
- The following identities are [[factoring]] formulas; more general [[factoring]] formulas are listed in the wiki [Algebraic Manipulation - Identities](https://brilliant.org/wiki/algebraic-manipulation-identities/#algebraic-manipulation-identities-medium "Algebraic Manipulation - Identities"):
$$
\begin{aligned}
&x^{2}-y^{2}=(x+y)(x-y) \\
&x^{3}-y^{3}=(x-y)\left(x^{2}+x y+y^{2}\right) \\
&x^{3}+y^{3}=(x+y)\left(x^{2}-x y+y^{2}\right) \\
&x^{4}-y^{4}=\left(x^{2}-y^{2}\right)\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Three-variable Identities
The following identities can be derived by some clever [[factoring]] and manipulation of the terms:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x+y)(x+z)(y+z) &=(x+y+z)(x y+x z+y z)-x y z \\
x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2} &=(x+y+z)^{2}-2(x y+x z+y z) \\
x^{3}+y^{3}+z^{3}-3 x y z &=(x+y+z)\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}-x y-x z-y z\right) .
\end{aligned}
$$
