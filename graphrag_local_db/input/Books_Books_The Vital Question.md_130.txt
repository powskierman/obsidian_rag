the possible environments on the early earth that were conducive to the origin of life. We can distinguish between different types of gene, known as orthologs and paralogs. Orthologs are basically the same genes doing the same job in different species, inherited from a common ancestor, as in the example we’ve just considered. So all eukaryotes have an ortholog of the gene for citrate synthase, which we all inherited from our common ancestor. The second group of genes, paralogs, also share a common ancestor, but in this case the ancestral gene was duplicated within the same cell, often on multiple occasions, to give a gene family.
