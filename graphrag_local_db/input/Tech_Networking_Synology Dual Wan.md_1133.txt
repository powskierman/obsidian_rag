# Synology Dual Wan
Date: 2024-10-16

### Notes
You can configure the **Synology RT2600ac router** to use both your **Cogeco cable modem** and the **Nokia FastMile 5G gateway** for **WAN redundancy**. This setup ensures uninterrupted internet by automatically switching to the 5G network when your cable internet goes down. Below are the steps to achieve **dual WAN failover** on the Synology router.

  

**Step 1: Physical Setup**

1. **Connect WAN 1**:

• Use an Ethernet cable to connect the **Cogeco modem** to the **WAN port** on the Synology RT2600ac.

2. **Connect WAN 2 (5G Backup)**:

• On the **Nokia FastMile gateway**, connect an Ethernet cable to **one of its LAN ports**.

• Plug the other end into the **LAN 1 port** of the Synology RT2600ac (this will act as the secondary WAN).

**Step 2: Configure WAN Settings on Synology RT2600ac**

1. **Access the Synology Admin Panel**:

• Go to http://192.168.0.1 in your browser and log in to your Synology router.

2. **Enable Dual WAN**:

• Navigate to **Network Center > Internet > Connection**.

• Click **Add > Dual WAN**.

3. **Set WAN 1 as Primary Connection**:

• In the **Connection Priority** section, select **WAN (connected to Cogeco)** as the primary WAN.

• Choose **LAN 1 (connected to the Nokia gateway)** as the **secondary WAN**.

4. **Configure Failover Mode**:

• In the **Smart WAN settings**, select **Failover** mode. This ensures that the 5G connection will activate only if the Cogeco internet goes down.

• You can also set **Load Balancing** mode if you want both connections to share the load (not common for backups).

**Step 3: Nokia Gateway Configuration**

1. **Assign a Static IP to the Synology Router**:

• Use the **Nokia WiFi app** to assign a **DHCP reservation** to the Synology router on the Nokia’s LAN network.

• This ensures that the Nokia always gives the Synology router the same IP address.

2. **Set Up DMZ (Optional)**:

• To avoid double NAT issues, place the Synology router in the **DMZ** on the Nokia gateway.

**Step 4: Testing and Monitoring**

1. **Simulate Internet Downtime**:

• Unplug the Cogeco modem to test if the Synology router switches to the 5G connection.

• Ensure your devices remain connected to the internet during the failover.

2. **Check WAN Status**:

• In **Network Center > Internet > Status**, monitor the WAN connections to ensure failover is working correctly.

  

**Considerations**

• **5G Data Plan**: Ensure that your 5G data plan can handle extended use during downtime.

• **QoS Settings**: You may want to adjust **Quality of Service (QoS)** settings on the Synology router to manage bandwidth effectively.

• **Monitoring**: Use Synology’s **network monitoring tools** to track WAN usage and performance.

This configuration gives you **automatic WAN failover** between the Cogeco modem and the Nokia FastMile gateway. In the event of a cable outage, the Synology router will seamlessly switch to 5G until the primary connection is restored. This way, you get uninterrupted internet with the added security of redundancy.
