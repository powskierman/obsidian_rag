# Highlights

Old social skills and the ability to make customary social remarks are often retained longer than insight and judgment. Thus, a person may sound fine to the doctor but in fact be unable to care responsibly for himself. ([846](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=846)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464068

The person who has dementia probably feels lost, worried, anxious, vulnerable, and helpless much of the time. ([862](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=862)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464069

---

Accept that lost skills are gone for good (the woman who has lost the ability to cook will not learn to fix a meal), ([933](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=933)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464070

---

Consider having a trained person come to the home to visit the person who has dementia or trying a group program such as day care designed for people who have dementia ([944](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=944)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464071

with an illness that causes a progressive dementia, the person will eventually be unable to read or will not be able to make sense of what he reads. ([965](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=965)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464072

---

As the disease progresses, the person will be unable to remember what you tell him even for a minute. ([967](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=967)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464073

---

People with brain diseases often become excessively upset and may experience rapidly changing moods. ([983](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=983)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464074

---

Often a catastrophic reaction does not look like behavior caused by an illness that causes dementia. The behavior may look as if the person is merely being obstinate, critical, or overemotional. ([996](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=996)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464075

---

Sometimes catastrophic reactions and forgetfulness are the first behaviors family members see when they begin to realize that something is wrong. ([1001](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1001)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464076

---

First, you must fully accept that these behaviors are not just stubbornness or nastiness but a response that the person with dementia cannot help. ([1005](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1005)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464077

---

Take things one step at a time, and give instructions or information step by step. ([1024](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1024)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464078

---

Give the person who has dementia time to respond. ([1027](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1027)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464079

---

The key is to simplify, to reduce the number of signals the impaired, disoriented brain must sort out. ([1030](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1030)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464080

---

A good rule is to let a person do for himself until he shows the first signs of frustration, then assist him before he becomes more upset. ([1039](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1039)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464081

---

As a person who has dementia becomes upset, his ability to think and reason temporarily declines even more. It is useless to argue with him, explain things to him, or even ask him to complete a task when he is in the grip of a catastrophic reaction. Arguing, explaining, or restraining him may make things worse. ([1053](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1053)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464082

---

Combativeness is almost always an extreme catastrophic reaction. It often can be prevented by being alert to the person’s signals that his stress level is rising. ([1085](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1085)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464083

---

When a person becomes agitated, immediately stop whatever is upsetting him and let him relax. Do not continue to push him. ([1092](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1092)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464084

---

Some people begin to use curse words, even if they have never used such language before. This disturbing behavior appears to be a strange quirk of diseases that take away important language skills. ([1125](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1125)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464085

---

If the person is having difficulty finding the right word, it is usually less frustrating for him to have you supply the word for him than it is to let him search and struggle for the word. ([1138](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B005LHO8VE&location=1138)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334464086
