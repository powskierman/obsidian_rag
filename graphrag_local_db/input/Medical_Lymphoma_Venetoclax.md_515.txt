### Notes
-  Venetoclax is not currently approved as a first-line treatment with [[R-CHOP]] for high-grade B-cell lymphoma (HGBCL) in Alberta.  Evidence from the CAVALLI trial suggests that venetoclax combined with [[R-CHOP]] in the first-line setting improves outcomes, particularly in [[BCL2]]-positive subgroups, with a complete response rate of 69% and trends toward better progression-free survival compared to [[R-CHOP]] alone. ^9ff4b4
