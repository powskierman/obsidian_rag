### Notes
[Complete the square: Circles](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/sqrcircle.htm)
- Completing the square is used to turn a quadratic into the sum of a squared binomial and a number: $(x – a)^2 + b$. The center-radius form of the circle equation is in the format $(x – h)^2 + (y – k)^2 = r^2$, with the center being at the point (h, k) and the radius being "r".
