### Notes
-  When using a KitchenAid mixer with a dough hook to knead your baguette dough, follow these guidelines for optimal results:
	1.	Speed Setting: Use speed 2 on your KitchenAid mixer. This speed is recommended because it provides enough momentum to effectively knead the dough without overworking the motor.
	2.	Kneading Time:
	•	Start by mixing the dough for about 2 minutes to ensure all ingredients are well combined.
	•	Continue kneading for an additional 2 to 4 minutes on speed 2. This duration is generally sufficient to develop the gluten structure needed for baguettes.
	3.	Monitoring Dough:
	•	Keep an eye on the dough as it kneads. It should form a cohesive ball and clean the sides of the bowl. If it starts climbing up the hook or appears too sticky, stop and adjust as necessary.
	•	Perform a windowpane test after kneading to check if the gluten is well-developed. If not, you can knead for an additional minute or two, but be cautious not to over-knead.
By following these steps, you can efficiently knead your baguette dough using a KitchenAid mixer, ensuring a smooth and elastic dough ready for shaping and baking.
