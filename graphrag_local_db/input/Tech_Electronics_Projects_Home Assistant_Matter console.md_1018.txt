### Notes
-  `matter help` 
	- list all available commands.
-   `matter onboardingcodes ble`  
    - print pairing QR code.
-   `matter wifi connect "network" "password"`  
    - Configure Wi-Fi on the device
-   `matter device factoryreset`  
    - factory reset device
