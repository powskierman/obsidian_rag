If you see a red, yellow, magenta, etc., color warning triangle (rather than white), it’s not great, but it’s not nearly as bad as white. It means you’re clipping just that one color channel (and there’s still detail in the other channels). — location: [535](kindle://book?action=open&asin=B00PISVNAU&location=535) ^ref-37433

Do the same with the Blacks slider. — location: [595](kindle://book?action=open&asin=B00PISVNAU&location=595) ^ref-26541
