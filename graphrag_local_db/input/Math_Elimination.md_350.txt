### Notes
-  In algebra, the elimination method is the process of eliminating a variable by adding or subtracting, depending on whether the coefficients are opposites or equals, thus *eliminating* a variable which creates a new equation that is possible to solve. 
	- Example:$$
\left\{\begin{array}{l}
3 x-5 y=-9 \\
4 x+5 y=23
\end{array}\right.
$$
- add the two equations, which eliminates the _y_-term and get _x_ = 2.
