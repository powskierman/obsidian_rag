### Notes
- **Vector Search **🔍
  Best for:
  - Direct fact retrieval from your personal notes
  - Timeline creation from your documented experiences
  - Specific document content (like your PET scan results)
  - Personal medical history questions
  - Finding exact quotes or references from your vault

  Your use case: "Create a timeline of my lymphoma treatment" ✅

  **Graph Modes** 🌐
  Graph-Local 📍
  Best for:
  - Connecting related concepts within a specific topic area
  - Exploring relationships between entities you've documented
  - "What's connected to X?" type questions

  Example: "What treatments are connected to my CAR-T therapy experience?"

  Graph-Global 🌍
  Best for:
  - High-level synthesis across your entire knowledge base
  - Complex analytical questions that require reasoning across multiple
  domains
  - "Big picture" insights that connect disparate topics
  - Research-style questions that benefit from conceptual understanding

  Good examples:
  - "What patterns emerge across all my medical experiences?"
  - "How do my treatment decisions relate to my research on lymphoma
  options?"
  - NOT good: "What were my PET scan results?" (too specific/factual)

  Graph-Hybrid ⚡
  Best for:
  - Questions requiring both facts AND analysis
  - When you want comprehensive answers that combine specific details with
  broader insights
  - Complex multi-part questions

  Example: "Based on my treatment history, what should I consider for future
   therapy decisions?"

  Graph-Naive 🌐

  Best for:
  - Simple relationship exploration
  - Quick concept connections
  - When other graph modes are too slow

  The Key Insight:

  Graph modes excel at reasoning and synthesis, but can "hallucinate"
  general knowledge instead of using your vault content.

  Vector search excels at finding and retrieving your actual documented
  content.

  For your lymphoma timeline question: Vector search was the right choice
  because you wanted specific facts from your personal medical records, not
  general medical knowledge synthesis.

⏺ Rule of thumb: Start with vector search for personal factual queries, then
   use graph modes when you need analysis, connections, or synthesis across
  topics.
