2.  Showing and hiding fileextensions ([How To Show Desktop In Mac OS X](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbgRLeMH6QM))



1.  Step 1: Open a new**Finder**window on your Mac.

2.  Step 2: In the Menu bar, go to**Finder**> Preferences.

3.  Step 3: Click on the Advanced tab.

4.  Step 4: Select the box that says "Show all filename**extensions**". If you want to hide file**extensions**, just uncheck the box.
