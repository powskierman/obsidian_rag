### Notes
-  What is GPT-4o with tasks?
Tasks are, as the name suggests, scheduled actions ChatGPT will perform on your behalf without the need for you to be online and logged in. So they are basically small very simplified AI agents. Yes, you heard that right, I am saying AI agents!
