### Notes
- Pythagorian Identity: $\sin^2(\theta)+\cos^2(\theta)=1$
	- $\sin(\theta)=y$
	- $\cos(\theta)=x$
	- Definition of : $x^2+y^2=1$
	- Can be used for rewriting trigonometric expressions in equivalent, more useful, forms.
	-  Allows us to convert between the sine and cosine values of an angle, without knowing the angle itself.
