### Notes
- Set up Kobo Touch Extended, which is the plugin that will convert your epub to kepub. After you set up the plugin, you'll connect your eReader to your computer and click "Send to Device". The plugin should automatically convert the epub to kepub, but calibre won't store the kepub on your computer to save space. It'll only be on your kobo, and you can see the file type in "list mode" (it'll appear on the right side)

You can send the files in bulk by holding CTRL + Click on each book row, or CTRL + A (for all of your books), or CTRL + Shift + Clicking on two books to select every book between two rows. After that, click "Send to Device" and tadaaa it's been sent over!

To download the plugins, go to Preferences > Plugins > Get new plugins > (type the plugin names of the plugins below) > install. The links below have more information on setting them up

Plugins you’ll need:

Kobo Utilities (will back up your kobo information): [GUI Plugin] Kobo Utilities - MobileRead Forums (customize by going to the kobo utilities icon > downwards arrow > customize plugin...)
Kobo touch extended: [Device Plugin] Kobo Touch Extended Driver - MobileRead Forums (customize by going to preferences > plugins > search for "extended" > customize plugin)
Kobo touch extended requires these too: [Metadata Reader Plugin] KePub Metadata Reader - MobileRead Forums / [Metadata Writer Plugin] KePub Metadata Writer - MobileRead Forums (shouldn't need any customization)
If you want the metadata to be automatically updated when you connect your device, go to Preferences > Sending books to Device > Metadata management > Automatic Management
