4 Secrets to a Smoke Ring No matter what cooker you use, the secrets to a great smoke ring are all related to moisture: 1. Maintain high humidity in the cooker to keep a moist surface on the meat that will attract smoke. A water pan helps. 2. Maintain a steady, low temperature of about 225°F to minimize drying on the surface of the meat. 3. Add water manually by basting or spritzing the meat. Spritzing with apple juice or vinegar is a popular method. 4. Start with cold meat. Water vapor will condense on the cold surface like it does on a beer can on a sultry July day.
