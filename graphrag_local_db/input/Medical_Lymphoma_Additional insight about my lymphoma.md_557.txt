### Disease Status Assessment
- I have a persistent retroperitoneal nodal mass (conglomerate in the abdomen) with intense hypermetabolism (SUV max 27.4)
- This single site is rated Deauville 5, indicating it remains significantly more metabolically active than my liver (which has SUV 3.5)
- There are signs of partial response to R-CHOP as indicated by:
    - Slight size reduction in part of the mass (7.5 cm vs 9.0 cm previously)
    - Areas showing necrosis/cystic changes
    - No new hypermetabolic lesions
- Complete metabolic response in the right humeral lesion (SUV decreased from 11.0 to 1.7)
### Clinical Context
- My double-hit DLBCL did not achieve complete response after 4 cycles of R-CHOP
- This is consistent with the known resistance of double-hit lymphomas to standard chemoimmunotherapy
- The decision to switch to Yescarta is appropriate given the persistent disease
### Remission Potential with Yescarta
- For patients like me with double-hit DLBCL and partial response to prior therapy:
    - Complete response rates with Yescarta: approximately 40-50%
    - Durable remission potential (2+ years): approximately 30-35%

- <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">No evidence of disease progression</mark>

Given these findings, I have a reasonable chance of responding to Yescarta therapy. The persistent retroperitoneal mass with Deauville 5 uptake clearly indicates the need for a different treatment approach, but the limited disease burden and partial response to prior therapy are encouraging factors for potential success with CAR T-cell therapy.

### Summary:
- Double-hit lymphoma (DHL) is a high-risk subtype of large B-cell lymphoma characterized by concurrent rearrangements of the MYC and BCL2 genes. The aggressive nature of DHL stems from these <mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">genetic abnormalities, which drive proliferation (MYC) and inhibit apoptosis (BCL2)</mark>. Treatment strategies for DHL are evolving, with a focus on intensive chemotherapy regimens and novel therapies for relapsed/refractory cases.
