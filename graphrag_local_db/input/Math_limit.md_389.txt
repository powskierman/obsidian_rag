### Notes
- A [[function]] has a limit L when when $f(x)$ at point Q gets closer to $f(x)$ at point P as point Q gets closer to P. ![[Derivative.png]]
- 
- A [[derivative]] is calculated using the limit: $f'(P) =$$\lim\limits_{Q \to P}$$f(Q)-f(P) \over Q-P$
- simplest limits
	-  $\lim \over{x\to a}$ = $a$
	- ${\lim \over{x \rightarrow 0}} \frac{\sin x}{x}=1$
	-   $\lim \over{x\to a} $ $c$ = $c$ where c is a constant
	-  $\lim \over{x\to \infty}$$1\over x$ =  $\lim \over{x\to -\infty}$$1\over x$ = $0$
	- if $p(x)$ is a polynomial than  $\lim \over{x\to a}$$p(x) = p(a)$ 
- Limits of rational expression and asymptotic behaviour
	-  $\lim \over{x\to \infty}$${3x^3+2x^2-x+7}\over{2x^3-7x^2+3x-4}$
		- as it's a rational function, it's asymptotic, and the most significant terms by far are $cx^3$
		- Ditch the rest of the function
			- $\lim \over{x\to \infty}$${3x^3 \over 2x^3}$
		- Cancel like terms
			- $\lim \over{x\to \infty}$$3\over2$
- Squeeze limit law
	- if $g(x)\le f(x)\le h(x)$
	- for all $x$ near $x=a$ and
	- $\lim \over{x\to a}$$g(x)$= $\lim \over{x\to a}$$h(x)=L$ than:
	- $\lim \over{x\to a}$$f(x)$ = $L$

- ### Notation
	- limit of $x$ as it approaches $a$
		- $\lim \over{x\to a}$
	-  limit of $x$ as it approaches $a$ from above
		- $\lim \over{x\to a^+}$
	- limit of $x$ as it approaches $a$ from below
		- $\lim \over{x\to a^-}$
