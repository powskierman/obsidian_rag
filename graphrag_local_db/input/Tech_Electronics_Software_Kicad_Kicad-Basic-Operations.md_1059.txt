12. Etching with toner transfer

    a.  Print from PCBNew with mirrored print

    b.  Print to laser printer using Heat toner transfer paper

    c.  Clean copper clad with steel wool or Dremel brass brush and acetone

    d.  Tape printout to copper clad using masking tape

    e.  Put through laminator on thickest setting

    f.  Prepare a solution of 30cc H~2~O, 30cc HCL and 10cc H~2~O~2~

        a.  Water first, then acid, then peroxide
