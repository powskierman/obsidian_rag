### Notes
-  I have been diagnosed with High-Grade B-Cell Lymphoma (HGBL) with double-/triple-hit features, which means it has aggressive characteristics and requires urgent attention. Here's a summary of the potential treatment options if R-CHOP and #ASCT aren't effective:

1. **#CAR-T Cell Therapy**:
   - *Yescarta* (Axicabtagene ciloleucel): This is currently the best option based on available data. It has shown excellent response rates and durability in patients with high-grade and double-/triple-hit lymphomas.
   - *Breyanzi* (Lisocabtagene maraleucel) and *Kymriah* (Tisagenlecleucel): These are also viable options, but Yescarta has the most robust data for my specific situation.

2. **Bispecific Antibodies**:
   - *Epcoritamab*, *Glofitamab*, and *Mosunetuzumab*: These can be effective even in heavily pretreated patients and may allow outpatient treatment.

1. **[[BCL2]] Inhibitors**:
   - *Venetoclax* is a potential addition to my therapy, often used in combination with chemotherapy or targeted therapies like rituximab or obinutuzumab.

4. **Novel Targeted Therapies**:
   - Consider *selinexor*, *pirtobrutinib*, and other emerging agents under investigation for relapsed/refractory aggressive lymphomas.

1. **Epigenetic and [[MYC]]-Targeting Therapies**:
   - *BET inhibitors* and *CDK9 inhibitors* are being explored to target [[MYC]] transcription and expression in my type of lymphoma.

6. **Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs)**:
   - *Polatuzumab vedotin* could be considered, especially if I've relapsed or refractory to other treatments.

7. **Checkpoint Inhibitors**:
   - *Pembrolizumab* may be effective when combined with other therapies, particularly if my lymphoma has a high tumor mutational burden (TMB) or PD-L1 expression.

8. **Clinical Trials**:
   - Given the aggressiveness of my lymphoma, consider enrolling in clinical trials investigating dual-target #CAR-T cells, CAR-NK cells, and combination therapies involving venetoclax, bispecific antibodies, or checkpoint inhibitors.
