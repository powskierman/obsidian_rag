From Noether’s Theorem, this leads to conservation of momentum. ([3859](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B009VMBWAU&location=3859)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465198

Rotational Symmetry: The laws of physics don’t change if you rotate a system in its entirety. On large scales, this is reflected in the isotropy of the universe and is also part of the assumptions underlying the Cosmological Principle. This leads to conservation of angular momentum. Lorentz Invariance: The laws of physics look equally valid for any observer moving at constant speed and direction. It’s also the basis for Special Relativity. ([3863](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B009VMBWAU&location=3863)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334465200
