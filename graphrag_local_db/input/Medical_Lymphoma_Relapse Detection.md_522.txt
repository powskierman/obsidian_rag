### Notes
- The latest evidence supports three optimal biomarkers for predicting relapse risk and guiding individualized surveillance after Yescarta (axicabtagene ciloleucel) infusion in double hit lymphoma: circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) clearance, CAR-T cell quantification, and B-cell aplasia.
- ctDNA clearance is the most robust predictor. Rapid disappearance of ctDNA within weeks after infusion is strongly associated with durable remission, while persistent or re-emergent ctDNA predicts early relapse. Highly sensitive assays (e.g., MAESTRO, PhasED-Seq, clonoSEQ) can detect minimal residual disease at parts-per-million levels, enabling ==detection of molecular relapse weeks to months before clinical or radiographic progression.== Patients with undetectable ctDNA at 1–3 months post-infusion have markedly superior progression-free and overall survival, and ==serial ctDNA monitoring can identify high-risk patients who may benefit from preemptive intervention==.[1
