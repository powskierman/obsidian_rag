### Notes
- Limits respect arithmetic operations: 
	-  limits respect addition and subtraction
		-  [[limit]] of the sum is the sum of the limits
		-  [[limit]] of the difference is the difference of the limits
		- $\lim(f(x) ± g(x))$ = $(\lim f(x)) ± (\lim g(x))$
	-  limits respect multiplication
		-  [[limit]] of the product is the product of the limits
		- $\lim(f(x)g(x))$ = $(\lim f(x))(\lim g(x))$
		- special case:
			-  constants come out to the front
				-  $\lim kg(x))$ =$k \lim g(x)$
	-  limits respect taking quotients
		- [[limit]] of the quotient is the quotient of the limits
		- $\lim(f(x)/g(x))$ = $(\lim f(x))/(\lim g(x))$
			- **never divide by zero!**
