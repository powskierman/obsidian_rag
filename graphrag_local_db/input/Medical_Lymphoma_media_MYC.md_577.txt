### Notes
- The **[[[MYC]] gene**, located on ==chromosome 8 (8q24), encodes a transcription factor that regulates cell proliferation, metabolism, DNA repair, and apoptosis.==
	•	In DHL, [[MYC]] undergoes chromosomal translocation, ==often fusing with immunoglobulin genes (e.g., IGH on chromosome 14)==. This leads to overexpression of [[MYC]], driving ==uncontrolled cell division and tumor growth.==
	•	[[MYC]] dysregulation alone can trigger apoptosis through activation of the TP53 pathway. However, ==in DHL, this apoptotic response is often suppressed due to concurrent [[BCL2]] rearrangements.==
