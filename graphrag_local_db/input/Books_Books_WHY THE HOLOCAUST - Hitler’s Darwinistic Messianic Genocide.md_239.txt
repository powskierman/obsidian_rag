How does Hitler describe the order of the world and of nature in this passage? He views the triumph of the strong in the struggle for existence as the most reasonable of all laws. ([2679](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=2679)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466180

---

One of the characteristic features of Darwinian theory is the emphasis on the survival of the species and the devaluation in the worth of the individual; this is a consequence of the fact that the life of an individual is trivial in comparison with the existence of a species as a whole. This idea also forms a significant part of Hitler’s belief:   “One may be repelled by this law of nature which demands that all living things should mutually devour one another. The fly is snapped up by a dragon-fly, which itself is swallowed by a bird, which itself falls victim to a larger bird. This last, as it grows old, becomes a prey to microbes, which end by getting the better of it. These microbes, in their turn, find their predestined ends. ([2705](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=2705)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466181

on the basis of his previous arguments, Hitler reaches the conclusion that the Aryan race is the only culture-creating race, and subsequently bases his interpretation of the whole of cultural and world history upon it. ([3602](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3602)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466188

---

These conclusions, reached by Hitler on the basis of his essential evolutionary understanding of history and well illustrated by his metaphor about “climbing an endless ladder” and by the thesis that “all occurrences in world history are only the expression of the races’ instinct for self-preservation”, have, unfortunately, far-reaching theoretical, as well as practical, consequences. Two conclusions, mentioned here by Hitler, follow from this understanding: that there is a real threat to the existence of culture and to the survival of the human world, and that a race which is not good is chaff. Both of these conclusions will, understandably, have far-reaching consequences. ([3610](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3610)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466189

---

Why did Hitler advocate racism? Because he advocated Darwinism. ([3621](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3621)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466190

---

At the same time, it follows from Darwinism that humans are just living creatures among other living creatures – they are simply at a higher level of evolution, thus the laws of nature also apply to them, including the principle of the emergence of species through mutation. Mankind, too, should be considered as a collection of different races, or, rather, species, which fight one another for survival as the rest of nature does. If mankind is composed of many races fighting for their place in the sun, then it is, necessarily, also the best one which will win. Races should not, therefore, mix since it would mean the degradation and overall weakening of the ones highest on the scale and would reduce their hope of survival. It is, therefore, in the interest of one’s people to be a racist. ([3625](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3625)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466191

---

Human culture constitutes a part of human history too, so respecting racial laws is a necessary condition for the preservation of human culture. It is a part of Hitler’s evolutionary interpretation of the world that human culture is created by the most evolved races and that these races would become extinct if racial laws were disrespected. ([3634](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3634)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466192

---

Race is therefore also the key to human culture. And since, according to Hitler, the Aryan race, which lies at the foundation of the most powerful western civilization, is the most advanced culture-creating race of all peoples, it is the survival of the Aryan which becomes the most important thing from the standpoint of the whole of mankind. ([3638](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3638)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466193

The magnitude of the self-preservation instinct corresponds to the two most powerful motivations in life: hunger and love. While the [fulfillment] satisfaction of the eternal hunger guarantees self-preservation, the gratification of love secures its furtherance. In truth, these two impulses are the rulers of life. ([3683](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3683)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466194

---

The same laws that determine the life of the individual, and to which it is subject, are therefore valid for the people. Self-preservation and continuity are the major impulses for any kind of behavior, ([3692](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3692)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466195

---

We can also perceive Hitler’s theory on the essence of history between the lines of his claim that everything made of flesh and bone cannot ever withdraw itself from the principles that catalyzed its formation. In other words, all that we can find in human society, all the facts of human life as well as all spiritual abilities are the result of the evolutionary process and its laws. Ultimately, all we know originated from inorganic and later organic nature operating according to these laws and thus all must rightfully abide by these laws. When we apply such an idea to a human, representing a being of spirit and flesh, it makes the spirit appear as some kind of addition to the body. The substance, in this case, is the flesh and blood, and not the spirit having a body. Thus, the destruction of the body implies automatically the end of existence; immortality is inapplicable. Even love, according to Hitler’s evolutionary logic, can be reduced to a simple instinct which serves self-preservation and is degraded to a teleological instrument of Nature’s development. ([3722](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3722)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466196

---

Hitler resents the pacifist approach of culturally advanced peoples and their reluctance to conquer new territory. ([3797](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3797)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466197

---

If we come to understand, according to Hitler’s ideological basis, that everything is directed toward survival and serves the instinct for self-preservation, then it must also include the entire cultural activity of individual peoples. Each cultural creation can be viewed as an instrument applicable to the struggle for survival, because it raises the chances of a given people in a competitive battle. A technologically developed culture, for example, will have a greater chance of winning. In Hitler’s conception, the cultural standard of a people corresponds substantially with its racial level; therefore, a national culture is, in effect, a manifestation of its racial value. It is the image of a people’s racial quality. ([3958](https://readwise.io/to_kindle?action=open&asin=B0056BSJLA&location=3958)) %% Color: yellow %% ^334466198
