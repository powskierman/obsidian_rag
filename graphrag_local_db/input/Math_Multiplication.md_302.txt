### Notes
- The product of two real [[Numbers]] is negative if and only if one of the [[Numbers]] is positive and the other is negative.
- When encountering equations with fractions, eliminate the fractions by multiplying each side of the equation by the lowest common multiple of the denominators in that equation.
- Multiplication and areas of rectangles: 
	- The product ab may be represented, geometrically, by the area of a rectangle with side lengths a and b. [Notes: Equations and Inequalities](Notes-Equations-and-Inequalities.pdf)
