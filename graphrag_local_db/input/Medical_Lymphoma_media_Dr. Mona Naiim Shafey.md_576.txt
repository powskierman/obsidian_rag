### Notes
Dr. Mona Shafey is a prominent malignant hematologist and clinical researcher based in Calgary, Alberta. She specializes in treating blood cancers such as lymphoma and leukemia and is a leader in cellular therapies, including CAR T-cell therapy.
-   **Education and Career**: Dr. Shafey completed her medical degree and residencies in internal medicine and hematology at the University of Ottawa, graduating in 2008. She pursued a fellowship in cellular therapy at the University of Calgary and joined its staff in 2010. Since 2019, she has been the Director of the Alberta Blood and Marrow Transplant Program, overseeing cellular therapies like stem cell transplants and CAR T-cell treatments[1](https://albertacancer.ca/leap-magazine/research-rockstar-dr-mona-shafey/)[3](https://www.lymphoma.ca/educational-forum-car-t-cell-therapy-information-for-patients-in-alberta/).
-   **Current Roles**: She serves as the Clinical Director of the Alberta Cellular Therapy and Immune Oncology Initiative (ACTION) since 2022. ==This program develops next-generation therapies using patients' immune systems to combat cancer.== Dr. Shafey is also an Associate Professor at the University of Calgary[1](https://albertacancer.ca/leap-magazine/research-rockstar-dr-mona-shafey/)[4](https://profiles.ucalgary.ca/mona-shafey).  
-   **Research Contributions**: ==Dr. Shafey has been the principal investigator for 12 clinical trials, with six ongoing, focusing on CAR T-cell therapy and other innovative treatments for blood cancers.== She is leading a project using GCAR1, a CAR T-cell product developed at the University of Calgary, for rare cancers like alveolar soft part sarcoma (ASPS). Her team recently secured $3 million from the Canadian Institutes of Health Research for a multi-centre Phase 1 trial[1](https://albertacancer.ca/leap-magazine/research-rockstar-dr-mona-shafey/).
-   **Advocacy**: ==Dr. Shafey has played a critical role in expanding access to CAR T-cell therapy in Alberta.== She was instrumental in establishing local treatment programs at centers like the Tom Baker Cancer Centre in Calgary[3](https://www.lymphoma.ca/educational-forum-car-t-cell-therapy-information-for-patients-in-alberta/).
## **Impact in Alberta**

==Dr. Shafey's work has positioned Alberta as a leader in CAR T-cell therapy within Canada. The province offers both approved treatments for DLBCL and clinical trials for experimental therapies like GCAR1[1](https://albertacancer.ca/leap-magazine/research-rockstar-dr-mona-shafey/)[3](https://www.lymphoma.ca/educational-forum-car-t-cell-therapy-information-for-patients-in-alberta/). ==Her leadership ensures equitable access to these cutting-edge treatments for patients across Alberta.

Dr. Shafey continues to drive innovation in hematology, making significant strides in improving outcomes for patients with challenging cancers through cellular therapies like CAR T-cell treatment.
