- $y= \sin(x)$

- One can get the cosine curve from the sine curve by translating
the sine curve horizontally to the left by $\pi \over 2$ units 
	-  $\cos(x)={\sin(x)+{\pi \over 2}}$
	- $\sin(x)={\cos(x)-{\pi \over 2}}$
