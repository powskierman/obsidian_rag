```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Secrets("Secrets Configuration") -->|Load| A(HassRestClient)
    A -->|Initialize| B(URLSession)
    A -->|Uses| C(URL and Auth Token)
    
    E("API Endpoints") -.-> A
    F("HTTP Methods") -.-> A
    G("Request Body for POST") -.-> A
    
    A -->|Perform Request| H(URLSessionDataTask)
    B -.-> H
    C -.-> H
    G -.-> H
    
    H --> I(HTTP Response)
    I --> J{Success or Error?}
    J -->|Success| K(Decode JSON)
    J -->|Error| L(Handle Error)
    
    K --> M("Completion Handler Success")
    L --> M("Completion Handler Error")
    
    A -->|Define Actions| N("fetchDeviceState, sendCommandToDevice")
    N --> H
    ```
