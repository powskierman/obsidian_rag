1. **Personal knowledge management** () is a process of collecting information that a person uses to gather, classify, store, search, retrieve and share [knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge "Knowledge") in their daily activities
2. A  is a typical note that might contain a title, a paragraph describing a topic, and links to all of your other notes, tags, and maybe online resources related to it.
	1. Maps of Content offer a manual way of organizing notes
	2. They are "core" notes you can draft to make sense of the rest.
3. A Home note allows you to keep links to the most relevant notes in a central location.
4. use the **CTRL + N** keyboard shortcut to create a note.

9. To create an external link press **CMD+K** enter description in brackets and link in parenthesis.
10. To find a term within all notes **CMD**+**SHIFT**+F
11. Link notes:  In edit mode, press **CMD** and preview button.
12. You can press the default `CMD + E` key combination anytime to switch between editing and preview mode.
13. You can use tags to organize your notes. Like Twitter, you can type your tags anywhere you wish. #TAG or at the top of a note.
	1. Tags can be nested ex: #Rigol/Manual
14. You can seek specific tags using several tools:
	1. The search functionality (**CMD + F**). 
	2. The tag pane (which appears by default on the top-right if enabled). 
	3. The graph.
	4. it can detect mentions of a note's name, even if they aren't actual links.
15. Use the Backlinks pane on the right sidebar to see everything that connects back to the active note.
16. Add links to sites and online resources:
	1. Square brackets "Description of link" Parentheses "Link" Example: [Make Use Of Article ](https://www.makeuseof.com/turn-your-notes-into-a-second-brain-with-obsidian/)
17. To view a , click 3 dots in view mode and select "Start Presentation"
18. Embed both local content and online resources into your notes. Drag a PDF, image, or another markdown file into one of your notes, and Obsidian will automatically copy it into your vault and add a link to it. Example:!
	1. For online content, use "iframes":
		1. <iframe src="https://papierlos-studieren.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/logo-fat-discord-666x525.png"></iframe>
	2. An URL can be easilyconverted to iframe using the "Convert URL to Preview" community plugin.  Highlight the URL and press **OPT+CMD+I**
19. Organise Notes Using Folders
	1. There's a button for creating folders at the top of the navigation pane on the left sidebar. 
	2. You can freely create folders and subfolders and move notes between them.
20. Utilizing the Graph in Obsidian
	1. Press **CMD + G** to see a graph replacing your active note.
	2. The graph visually presents the links and tags between your notes
