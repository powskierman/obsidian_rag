- [[Hypotenuse]]
	- SOH: [[Sine]] = Opposite  over Hypotenuse $b \over c$
	- CAH: [[Cosine]] = Adjacent over Hypotenuse $a \over c$
	- TOA: [[Tangent]] = Opposite over Adjacent $b \over a$
	-  
- The remaining angle of the [[triangle]] is 90◦ − θ (since the angles of the [[triangle]] add up to 180◦)
	- $sin(90^{\circ}-\theta)$ = [[Cosine]]
		- ex: $\sin(70)=\cos(20)$
	- $cos (90^{\circ}-\theta)$ = [[Sine]]
	- $tan(90^{\circ}-\theta)$ = $1 \over \tan$ = $\cos (\theta) \over \sin(\theta)$ 
		- ex: $\tan(70)={1 \over \tan(20)}$
-  [[Circular Identity]]
	-  For any angle $θ$: $\sin^2(θ)+\cos^2(θ) = 1$
- [[Special Triangles]]
	- In a 30-60-90 triangle, the ratio of the sides is always in the ratio of 1:√3: 2
