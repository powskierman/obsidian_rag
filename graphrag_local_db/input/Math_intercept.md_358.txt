[x and y Intercepts](https://www.purplemath.com/modules/intrcept.htm)

- Whichever intercept you're looking for, the other variable gets set to zero. 
-  $y = 0$ for the $x$-intercept(s), so:
	- $25x^2 + 4y^2 = 9$
	- $25x^2 + 4(0)^2 = 9$
	- $25x^2 + 0 = 9$
	- $x^2 =  {9 \over25}$
	- $x = ± {3 \over 5}$
