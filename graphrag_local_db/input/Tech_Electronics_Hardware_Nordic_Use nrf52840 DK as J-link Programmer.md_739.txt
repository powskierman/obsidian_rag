### Notes
-  Connect the DK with the Dongle:
	- P20 VDD nrf to Dongle Vdd Out
	- P20 Vdd nrf to P20 SWD sel
	- P20 SWD IO to Dongle SWD IO
	- P20 SWD CLK to Dongle SWD CLK
	- DK GRD to Dongle GRD
- Connect DK to USB
- The MBR for nrf52840 can be found at:
