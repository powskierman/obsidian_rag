# Discontinuities
- ### Jump discontinuity
	- Both a left and right [[limit]] exist but are not equal
![[jump.png|400]]
- ### Infinite Discontinuity
	- The function doesn't approach a particular finite value, the [[limit]](https://www.mathwarehouse.com/calculus/limits/) does not exist.
![[infinite.png|400]]
- ### Removable discontinuity
	- Both left and right limits are equal
	- In the graphs below, there is a hole in the function at x=a. These holes are called *removable discontinuities*
![[Removable.png|400]]
