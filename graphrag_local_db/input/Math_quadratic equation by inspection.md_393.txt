### Notes
- 	- Look for numbers that add up to give the coefficient of $x$ and multiply to give the constant term.
	- Works best when the coefficient of $x^2$ is 1
	- Relies on the fact that:

$$(x+a)(x+b) = x^2 +(a+b)x+ab$$
	- Ex: $x2 + 4x − 12 = 0$
		- $x=6,-2$
