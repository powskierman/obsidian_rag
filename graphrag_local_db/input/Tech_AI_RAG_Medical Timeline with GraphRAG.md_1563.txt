### Main Idea

GraphRAG-Local-Ollama transforms medical knowledge management by automatically reconstructing treatment timelines, discovering causal relationships, and connecting clinical data across time - eliminating manual timeline reconstruction. **Now successfully deployed and actively indexing 1,607 vault files.**

### Treatment Timeline Example

**Query:** "How did my treatment progress from diagnosis to current status?"

**Traditional RAG Response:**
- Returns disconnected chunks:
  - "Diagnosed with DLBCL"
  - "Started R-CHOP chemotherapy"
  - "Received Yescarta CAR-T"
  - "3-month PET scan results"
  
User must manually construct timeline.

**GraphRAG-Local Response:**
```
Complete Treatment Graph:

2024-12: Diagnosis → DLBCL (high-grade, double-hit)
   ↓
2024-12 to 2025-03: R-CHOP (6 cycles)
   ├─ Response: Partial
   ├─ Side effects: Nausea, fatigue
   └─ Outcome: Residual disease detected
   ↓
2025-04: Decision → CAR-T therapy
   ├─ Factors: Refractory disease, double-hit, eligible
   ├─ Option chosen: Yescarta (axicabtagene ciloleucel)
   └─ Rationale: 70% durable response rate
   ↓
2025-04: Lymphodepletion → Preparation
   ↓
2025-04: Yescarta infusion
   ├─ Side effects: CRS grade 2, neurotoxicity grade 1
   ├─ Management: Tocilizumab, supportive care
   └─ Recovery: 2 weeks inpatient
   ↓
2025-07: 3-month PET scan
   ├─ Result: Complete metabolic response
   ├─ SUV max: Normalized
   └─ Status: No active disease
   ↓
2025-10: 6-month monitoring
   ├─ PET scan: Continued remission
   ├─ IgG levels: Low (B-cell aplasia)
   ├─ Intervention: IVIG therapy monthly
   └─ Status: Excellent response maintained
```

**Decision Support Example:**

**Query:** "What factors influenced my decision to do Yescarta over stem cell transplant?"

**Decision Graph:**
```
R-CHOP incomplete response
   → led to → consideration of alternatives
      → evaluated → [Stem cell transplant, CAR-T therapy]
         → stem cell transplant
            ├─ Pros: Established approach
            ├─ Cons: Age factor, risk, conditioning
            └─ Status: Deferred
         → CAR-T (Yescarta)
            ├─ Pros: Single infusion, 70% durable response
            ├─ Pros: Good for double-hit lymphoma
            ├─ Pros: Eligibility criteria met
            └─ Chosen ✓
               ← because → High-grade DLBCL
               ← because → Double-hit status
               ← because → Refractory to R-CHOP
               ← because → Strong evidence base
```
