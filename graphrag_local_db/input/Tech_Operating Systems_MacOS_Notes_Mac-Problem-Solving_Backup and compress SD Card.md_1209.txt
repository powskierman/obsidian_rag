### Notes
-  Insert SD and find out where it is mounted
	- diskutil list
- To compress it:
	- sudo dd if=/dev/disk9 bs=1m status=progress | gzip > ~/Desktop/sdcard.img.gz
-  Or if you don't want to compress it:
	- sudo dd if=/dev/disk9 of=~/Desktop/sdcard.img bs=1m status=progress
- To restore it, first unmount the SD
	- diskutil unmountDisk /dev/disk9
	- gzip -dc ~/Desktop/sdcard.img.gz | sudo dd of=/dev/disk9 bs=1m status=progress
- Or if it is not compressed:
	- sudo dd if=~/Desktop/sdcard.img of=/dev/disk9 bs=1m status=progress
