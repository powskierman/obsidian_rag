### Notes
-  a delegate is a powerful design pattern used for backward communication or for allowing an object to offload some of its responsibilities to another object. This pattern is fundamental in many frameworks and libraries, especially in iOS and macOS development with Swift and Objective-C.
- Example:  For instance, a `UITableView` has a `delegate` and a `dataSource` (which is also a form of delegate). These delegates are responsible for providing data to the table view and handling actions like cell selection.
- The `UITableView` doesn’t know (or need to know) where the data comes from or what to do when a user interacts with it. These decisions are delegated to other objects conforming to the `UITableViewDelegate` and `UITableViewDataSource` protocols.
