- Determine the domain and range of the given function:$$y=−\sqrt{−2x+3y}$$
- The domain is all values that _x_ can take on. 
	- No negative inside a square root. 
- Procedure:
		- Set the insides of $\sqrt{} $ $>=0$. That is the domain:
				- $–2x + 3 ≥ 0$
				- $–2x ≥ -3$
				- $2x ≤ 3$
				- $x ≤ 3/2 = 1.5$
				- The domain is "all _x_ ≤ 3/2".

- The range requires a graph:
	- The range is "all _y_ ≤ 0".
