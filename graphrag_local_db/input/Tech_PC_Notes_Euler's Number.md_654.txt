- $e$ is the maximum possible result of continuous compounding at 100%  rate over one period
-  The number $e$ is one of the most important numbers in mathematics.
- **_e_** is the base of the Natural [Logarithms](https://www.mathsisfun.com/sets/function-logarithmic.html) 
- **_e_** is an [irrational number](https://www.mathsisfun.com/irrational-numbers.html) (it cannot be written as a simple fraction).
- The first few digits are: 2.7182818284590452353602874713527
- Domain of $y=e^n$ is $\mathbb R$
- Range of $y=e^n$ is $\mathbb R^+=(0,\infty)$
- $\ln e^x=x$ for all $x \in \mathbb R$
- $e^{\ln x}=x$ for all $x>0$
- At any point the slope of $e^x$ equals the value of $e^x$ : 
	- when x=0, the value $e^x$ = 1, and the slope = 1
	- when x=1, the value  $e^x$ = e, and the slope = $e$  
	- etc...
	-  !
- The area **up to** any x-value is also equal to $e^x$ :
	!
- $a^b=e^{b \ln a}$
