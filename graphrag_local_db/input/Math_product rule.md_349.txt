[[derivative MOC]]
- Used when the [[derivative MOC|derivative]] of products of two functions is needed
-  The [[derivative MOC|derivative]] of the product of two functions is the first function times the [[derivative MOC|derivative]] of the second function plus the second function times the [[derivative MOC|derivative]] of the first function.
- $d \over dx$ `[`$f(x)g(x)$`]` = $f(x)g'(x)$ + $g(x)f'(x)$
-  

Example 1:
if $f(x) = xe^x$ find $f'(x)$

By the Product Rule we have:
$f'(xe^x)$ = $d \over dx$ $(xe^x)$ = $x$$d \over dx$$(e^x)$ + $e^x$$d \over dx$$(x)$ = $xe^x + e^x \cdot 1$ = $e^x(x+1)$
- [[Product of Square Root]]
	- Product of squaqre root is square root of product
