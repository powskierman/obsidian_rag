### Notes
[MathIsFun, Asymptote](https://www.mathsisfun.com/algebra/asymptote.html)

- asymptotes are lines which correspond to the zeroes of the denominator of a rational function. 
	- ex: For $f(x)= -1+{1\over{x-2}}$
		- The horizontal asymptote is -1
		- The vertical asymptote is 2
		- ![[Asymptote.jpg|500]]
- In the [[Cartesian Plane]] they are the $x$ and $y$ axis.
